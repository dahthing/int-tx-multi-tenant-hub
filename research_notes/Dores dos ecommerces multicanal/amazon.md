# Amazon Europe (amazon.es first): operational pains of Portuguese and Spanish SMB sellers, and what a multichannel hub could solve

> Method note (read first). Research date: 23 October 2026. WebFetch was tried once (developer-docs.amazon.com) and failed with a DNS error (`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND`), so **every finding below comes from web-search result summaries, not from full pages**. Snippets can lose context; treat exact numbers as "reported by the cited page" and re-check the high-stakes ones (fees, thresholds, dates) in Seller Central before using them in pricing or product decisions. 35 web searches were used. Where the evidence is US-only, anecdotal (seller forums), or from a vendor with a commercial interest, that is flagged inline.

## 1. Account health: metrics, thresholds, what happens on breach, how common suspensions are

### Takeaway
Amazon EU runs on a small set of hard thresholds (ODR < 1%, pre-fulfilment cancellation < 2.5%, late shipment < 4%, valid tracking > 95%). Since 15 July 2026 it has also enforced an FBM on-time delivery rate of at least 90% in ES, FR, IT and DE, and since 1 September 2026 it can deactivate listings that miss it. Those are all summarised in an Account Health Rating where 200+ is "healthy". About a third of surveyed sellers report having been suspended at least once, but that figure comes from a mostly US survey. Iberian evidence is anecdotal and points to KYC re-verification and opaque deactivations.

### Cited Findings
**Core seller-fulfilled (FBM) metrics, Amazon EU policy documents**
- Order Defect Rate (ODR): orders with negative feedback, A-to-z claims or chargebacks over a 60-day window. Above 1% "may result in loss or restriction of selling privileges." — [Amazon "Changes to Order Performance Program Policy" UK PDF](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/03/B2B/MFN/Changes_to_Order_Performance_Program_Policy_-_UK.pdf); [DE PDF](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/03/B2B/MFN/Changes_to_Order_Performance_Program_Policy_-_DE.pdf)
- Pre-fulfilment Cancellation Rate: seller-fulfilled orders only, seller-initiated cancellations over 7 days. Above 2.5% may lead to deactivation. — same Amazon policy PDFs; also [ecomengine guide (updated 16 Jun 2025)](https://www.ecomengine.com/blog/amazon-seller-performance-metrics)
- Late Shipment Rate: seller-fulfilled only, measured over 10 and 30 days. Target below 4%, and higher rates may lead to restrictions or deactivation of seller-fulfilled offers. — [ecomengine](https://www.ecomengine.com/blog/amazon-seller-performance-metrics); [SellerLabs ODR guide](https://www.sellerlabs.com/knowledge-base/order-defect-rate-explained-what-counts-and-how-to-lower-it/)
- Valid Tracking Rate: seller-fulfilled only, 30-day window, target above 95%. Falling below it in a category can restrict selling non-FBA products in that category. — [ecomengine](https://www.ecomengine.com/blog/amazon-seller-performance-metrics); [AdNabu](https://blog.adnabu.com/amazon/amazon-seller-metrics/)
- Conflicting anecdote: a Seller Central Europe forum poster claims VTR "will not cause your account to get suspended". This is community opinion, not policy. — [SC-EU forum "Delivery Performance question"](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/forums/t/delivery-performance-question/191930) / [SC-EU forum "On-Time Delivery Rate"](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/eff8ca71-3011-4a20-b17c-d3a76c1d0d8e) (the search summary did not identify which of the two threads)

**New in 2026: FBM On-Time Delivery Rate (OTDR) enforced in Spain**
- From **15 July 2026**, FBM sellers must keep an on-time delivery rate of **≥ 90% in DE, FR, IT and ES**. The rate is the share of FBM units delivered on or before the "Deliver by" date. From **1 September 2026**, if a seller stays below 90%, "non-compliant listings may be deactivated, or sellers may lose the ability to list new FBM products." — [Amazon SP-API changelog: EU FBM requirements](https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/changelog/sp-api-updates-eu-fbm-requirements-product-type-definitions-api-title-structure-external-fulfillment-api-shipment-status)
- Trade press describes the 15 July 2026 start for Germany and says similar changes apply in France, Italy and Spain. Timing details differ between sources. — [Cross-Border Magazine](https://cross-border-magazine.com/?p=13316); [ChannelEngine "Marketplace Talk: July 2026"](https://www.channelengine.com/en/blog/marketplace-talk-july)
- A separate 90% "business hour delivery rate" for FBM shipments to Amazon Business customers starts 30 September 2026, with possible deactivation of B2B offers after 30 October. **US-focused source; EU applicability not confirmed.** — [SellerSprite blog](https://www.sellersprite.com/en/blog/amazon-business-hour-delivery-rate-2026)

**Prime and premium shipping thresholds (conflicting, partly dated)**
- Seller Prime: a minimum on-time delivery rate of 92% "from October 1". The year is not visible in the snippet. — [SC-EU forum "Update to Seller Prime program requirements starting August 23"](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/e602fa0e-3b57-45eb-a671-0fb804eaff37)
- Seller Fulfilled Prime: ship ≥ 99% of SFP orders on or before the promised ship date (seller forum quote). — [SC-EU forum SFP performance-drop thread](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/d13053d8bfa6ec4dc8a99500d6ccb29c)
- Prime badging (2023 rule): the fastest 85% of eligible offers get the badge, with a delivery speed of 4 days or faster in DE/FR and **5 days or faster in Spain**. — [Amazon developer newsletter, June 2023](https://developer.amazonservices.com/newsletter-june2023); [SC-EU forum "Prime badge eligibility in much of EU"](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/ac97fce25ef77e2c84714f80cf958679)
- Premium Shipping eligibility requires OTDR ≥ 97%. — [SC-EU forum](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/cf24bf728fb8d9ff932ae22e2a6f3047)
- A 3PL vendor describes SFP in Europe as needing a 3PL to meet "strict" metrics (vendor interest). — [FlexFulfillment](https://www.flexfulfillment.eu/amazon-seller-fulfilled-prime-sfp-in-europe-how-to-use-a-3pl-to-meet-amazons-strict-sfp-performance-metrics/)

**Account Health Rating (AHR)**
- Scale 0–1,000, new accounts start at 200. 200–1,000 is "Healthy", 100–199 "At Risk", 0–99 "Unhealthy" and eligible for deactivation at any time. — [SellerLabs AHR guide](https://www.sellerlabs.com/knowledge-base/how-amazon-account-health-rating-ahr-works-the-complete-seller-s-guide/); [Repricer (ES)](https://www.repricer.com/es/blog/amazon-account-health/). **Conflict:** an Italian outlet places the deactivation-risk zone below 200. — [Agenda Digitale](https://www.agendadigitale.eu/?p=240293)
- Each open policy issue subtracts points, and fixing the root cause (for example removing the listing or an accepted Plan of Action) restores them. "Unhealthy" accounts get a 72-hour warning before possible deactivation. — [SellerSonar glossary](https://sellersonar.com/blog/glossary/account-health/)
- Account Health Assurance gives eligible sellers time to fix issues. It requires AHR ≥ 200, a Professional plan, at least one year of activity and a registered emergency phone number. — [WisePPC (ES)](https://wiseppc.com/es/blog/amazon-account-suspension-explained/)
- Seller confusion: one seller reports an AHR of 216 "yet metrics are perfect". — [SC-EU forum](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/ec668c4e02dbface5adb6904b3328b08)

**Suspensions and verification**
- SmartScout "Voice of the Amazon Seller 2025" (325 sellers): **35% had experienced an account suspension**, mid-sized sellers (about $100K–$1M revenue) were most affected, and almost two-thirds think Amazon is not a fair marketplace. **Sample is predominantly US; no Iberian breakdown.** — [Business Wire, 12 Mar 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250312917598/en/)
- A second-hand "over 35% of sellers received a notice of violation in 2024" figure has no named primary study and looks recycled from SmartScout. — [Canopy Management](https://canopymanagement.com/how-to-prevent-amazon-suspension/)
- KYC re-verification case (SC-EU forum): the seller received the request in Spanish, inventory sending and disbursements were limited, and Amazon asked for ID, company registration proof, bank statements, supply-chain documents and a verification video call. — [SC-EU forum thread](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/32cb2957-0707-41cd-8ce2-d79f2e19e88b)
- Seller Central forum testimonies from sellers on amazon.es describe suspensions or blocked listings without a clear explanation and slow support (anecdotal, undated). — [SC-EU forum "Amazon.es issues"](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/forums/t/amazon-es-issues/196317?page=3)
- The standard recovery route is a detailed Plan of Action (POA) in Seller Central, distinguishing metric issues, policy violations and counterfeit complaints (law-firm site). — [terms.law (ES)](https://terms.law/Demand-Letters/Spanish/es-amazon-suspension.html)

### Inferences
- For an Iberian FBM seller who also sells on its own PrestaShop store, the **cancellation rate (2.5%)** and the **new 90% OTDR** are the two metrics most directly broken by multichannel operations: overselling forces cancellations, and slow pick-pack or carrier delays break the delivery promise. These are exactly the metrics a hub's stock and order modules touch.
- The OTDR enforcement from 1 September 2026 is recent. Sellers using slow carriers to Portugal or the islands (Canarias/Baleares) or long handling times are newly exposed. This is a timely hook for a hub selling "delivery-promise" monitoring.
- Suspension risk is high-impact but low-frequency per seller. A hub can only *warn* (metric trend alerts). It cannot handle appeals, which is agency/consultant territory.

### Gaps
- No Amazon-official EU page was retrieved confirming the current AHR bands or the exact Prime/SFP thresholds in Spain for 2026. The figures above are forum or third-party.
- No Iberia-specific statistic on suspension frequency was found.
- Whether the seller performance data (AHR, OTDR) is fully exposed via SP-API reports was not verified in this run.

## 2. Catalog and listings: GTIN/EAN, Brand Registry, hijacking, suppression, GPSR/EPR compliance, translations

### Takeaway
Listing on Amazon EU is now as much a compliance exercise as a content one. Sellers need a GTIN or an exemption per category, GPSR responsible-person data on every offer (enforced since 13 Dec 2024), EPR registration numbers per category and country, and translations for each EU store, which Amazon's own auto-translation often gets wrong. On top of that, hijacking and brand-name overwrites persist even for Brand Registry owners. Developers also lost the old flat-file/XML feeds in July 2025, and the JSON schemas that replaced them enforce more attributes.

### Cited Findings
- **GTIN exemption**: requested in Seller Central with product details and images showing no GS1 barcode and the brand permanently printed on the packaging. Amazon typically answers within about 48 hours, and the exemption must be requested **per category**. — [barcode.graphics](https://www.barcode.graphics/amazon-gtin-exemption/)
- Amazon keeps a list of brands that **require** a GTIN to list. Advice is that brand builders should buy GS1 codes rather than rely on the exemption. — [Practical Ecommerce](https://www.practicalecommerce.com/sell-on-amazon-without-gtins-or-upcs)
- **Hijacking / brand overwrites (SC-EU forums, anecdotal)**: brand owners report other brands appearing on their detail pages. In one case the brand was registered but the owner could not edit the page (error 5995). Amazon moderators say action was taken but "cannot give details" because it involves another seller. — [SC-EU forum "Our Brand Name has been changed / hijacked listings"](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/f87d2882-5895-4f67-935d-a442a8b40dc9); [SC-EU forum "Fake Brand stole listing of product we have sold for years"](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/4e812a5d-3002-450b-aa16-02c74bf0fea4)
- After a brand fix, the hijacker complained and Amazon reverted to the wrong brand. — [SC-EU forum "Hijacker reversed brand name change"](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/3a87edce-b9f8-43ee-9e45-dd78c38e5b04)
- **GPSR (EU General Product Safety Regulation)**: "as of December 13, 2024, all offers must comply with GPSR requirements, and non-compliant offers may be subject to removal". The responsible person must be in the EU/NI (manufacturer, importer, authorised representative, or fulfilment service provider). — [M2E Pro blog](https://blog.m2epro.com/gpsr-requirements-what-s-new-for-amazon-and-ebay-sellers-in-the-eu/); [FlexFulfillment](https://www.flexfulfillment.eu/compliance-gpsr-obligations/)
- GPSR pain example: one seller says Amazon would remove **3,900 of their EU listings** on 13 December and "refuse to engage on the responsible person issue". Amazon's list of about 50 responsible persons matched none of their manufacturers. — [SC-EU forum "GPSR Exit from EU Selling"](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/ef772626-6707-481f-8ab4-7863a338affe)
- Automated compliance checks suppress listings without a valid EU RP name and address (3PL vendor claim). — [FlexFulfillment](https://www.flexfulfillment.eu/compliance-gpsr-obligations/)
- **EPR in Spain**: Amazon.es applies EPR requirements to sellers **not established in Spain** who are "producers". Packaging EPR has applied since 1 Jan 2023, and since Oct 2024 it also covers tobacco with plastic filters and batteries. A separate EPR registration number is needed per product category. If none is provided, Amazon declares and pays the eco-fees by default and recovers them from the seller. Non-established sellers must also appoint an authorised representative, and offers can be suspended for non-compliance. — [ChannelX, Oct 2023](https://channelx.world/2023/10/epr-requirements-in-spain-for-amazon-sellers/); [Ecosistant](https://www.ecosistant.eu/en/epr-registration-number-amazon/)
- From 27 Aug 2025 the EPR "pay on behalf" policy for UK, Spain and France was unified into one policy (compliance vendor). — [AVASK (ES)](https://avask.com/es/?p=22315)
- **Translations / Build International Listings (BIL)**: one seller syncing from UK to FR/IT/DE/ES found Amazon's automatic translations wrong, and manual title edits had not appeared after several days. — [SC-EU forum "Automatic translation issues and no support from amazon"](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/077c320f03dc23b2d7cd454f393e0e2c)
- Another seller had listings copied and translated into EU stores while BIL was switched off, which triggered GPSR warnings. — [SC-EU forum](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/3143e1b6-171a-451a-bbdf-020fb7cc2cde)
- Only 2 of about 15 ASINs transferred via BIL (UK→US), the rest errored. — [SC-EU forum](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/f99cbc650423515e97b6ebc6bc20c38e)
- "Many sellers complaining about their listings being mistranslated by the BIL tool". BIL only works where ASINs exist in the target store (dated **2019**). — [ChannelX 2019](https://channelx.world/2019/03/things-consider-using-build-international-listings-amazon/)
- Amazon extended its generative-AI listing creation tools to Europe in June 2024. — [TechCrunch, 20 Jun 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/06/20/amazon-extends-generative-ai-powered-product-listings-to-europe)
- **Developer-side listing change**: XML and flat-file listings feeds via the Feeds API stopped being accepted on **31 July 2025** (migration target was 31 Mar 2025). The replacements are the Listings Items API or `JSON_LISTINGS_FEED`. The Product Type Definitions JSON schemas "can require more attributes than the old category XSDs, including conditionally required ones". Seller Central uploads are not affected. — [SP-API deprecation notice](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/changelog/deprecation-of-feeds-api-support-for-xml-and-flat-file-listings-feeds); [Listings migration guide](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/listings-management-workflow-migration)
- The 2026 EU FBM changelog also bundles Product Type Definitions and **title structure** changes (title only seen in the URL/heading; details not retrieved). — [SP-API changelog](https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/changelog/sp-api-updates-eu-fbm-requirements-product-type-definitions-api-title-structure-external-fulfillment-api-shipment-status)

### Inferences
- For an Iberian SMB, a PIM that stores **GTIN or exemption status, GPSR responsible-person and safety info, EPR numbers per country and category, and native ES/PT/FR/IT/DE copy** per SKU, and validates against Amazon's JSON product-type schema *before* pushing, directly attacks suppressed listings, the most common catalog failure. The EPR point matters most for **Portuguese** sellers, who count as "not established in Spain" when selling on amazon.es (inference from the ChannelX/Ecosistant rule).
- AI translation is a natural hub feature, but the forum complaints are about Amazon's *auto*-translation and its slow propagation. A hub would still need native-quality review and edit tracking.
- Hijacking can only be *detected* by a hub (for example by watching LISTINGS_ITEM_STATUS_CHANGE notifications or detail-page attribute drift). Remediation remains with Brand Registry.

### Gaps
- No official Amazon page was retrieved on category approvals ("ungating") in Spain. Not covered.
- No measured frequency of suppressed listings for Iberian sellers was found.
- Whether Amazon relaxed or extended GPSR enforcement in 2026 is unknown.

## 3. Stock, fulfilment and shipping: FBA vs FBM vs SFP, Pan-EU FBA and VAT, capacity, overselling, delivery promise

### Takeaway
Portugal has no Amazon fulfilment centre, so Portuguese sellers sell on amazon.es and ship FBA stock into Spain or fulfil FBM themselves. Spain-based sellers can use Spanish FBA, Pan-European FBA (which triggers VAT registrations in every storage country) or Multi-Channel Fulfilment to serve their own web orders from FBA stock. The day-to-day pain for multichannel FBM sellers is overselling from slow stock sync. That feeds the 2.5% cancellation metric, and late delivery now feeds the 90% OTDR. Capacity-limit evidence is mostly US.

### Cited Findings
- **Portugal**: Amazon has no FBA warehouse in Portugal, and guides recommend selling via amazon.es. — [Eurosender PT guide](https://www.eurosender.com/pt/guia-vendas/amazon)
- Orders from Portuguese buyers on amazon.es ship from Spanish FBA sites (Madrid, Barcelona, Seville), with 2–4 day delivery and "no public date" for an amazon.pt launch (**vendor claim, unverified**). — [ZunaPro](https://www.zunapro.com/portugal/es/blog/vender-amazon-es-mercado-portugal)
- Amazon says more than 1,000 Portuguese SMEs sell on Amazon with over €145M in exports (main destinations ES, FR, DE, US, UK). An earlier release put 2022 exports at about €100M, with 38% of Portuguese sellers using Amazon logistics. Amazon has localised amazon.es, sell.amazon.es, Seller Central and Seller University for Portuguese. — [About Amazon EU: Portuguese sellers FC tour in Spain](https://www.aboutamazon.eu/news/empowering-small-business/portuguese-sellers-explore-growth-opportunities-during-amazon-fulfilment-centre-tour-in-spain); [About Amazon EU: €100M exports](https://aboutamazon.eu/news/empowering-small-business/portuguese-smes-record-export-sales-of-100-million-with-amazon); [Portugal Global](https://www.portugalglobal.pt/en/news/portuguese-smes-record-export-sales-of-100-million-with-amazon-1) (the summary did not say which release carries the €145M figure)
- A vendor claims Portuguese sellers reach about 83M Iberian consumers with one account, with seller approval in 3–7 business days (**vendor claim**). — [ZunaPro amazon.es-pt](https://www.zunapro.com/portugal/pt/mercado/amazon-es-pt)
- **Spain**: more than 17,000 Spanish SMEs exported over €1.2bn via Amazon in 2024 (+20% YoY). 75% sell outside Spain and 65% of those export to the EU, against an ICEX figure of 9% export rate for online SMEs generally. Amazon's target is €2bn by 2030 (self-reported). — [Amazon press ES, Oct 2025](https://press.aboutamazon.com/es/noticias/impacto-economico/2025/10/pymes-espanolas-venden-amazon-1-200-millones-euros-exportaciones-2024); [Amazon press ES, 30 Sep 2025](https://press.aboutamazon.com/es/noticias/impacto-economico/2025/9/amazon-objetivo-duplicar-exportaciones-anuales-pymes-espanolas-2-000-millones-euros-2030). **Conflict:** Estrategias de Inversión attributes the €1.2bn to 2025. — [Estrategias de Inversión](https://www.estrategiasdeinversion.com/actualidad/noticias/otras/amazon-gana-290-millones-en-espana-en-2025-y-eleva-n-930667)
- **Pan-EU FBA and VAT**: OSS covers cross-border B2C sales, **not** stock stored in other countries. Each storage country needs its own VAT registration. Each cross-border stock transfer is a taxable event (deemed supply plus intra-EU acquisition), with no minimum: an €80 transfer creates the same obligations as €80,000. Because Amazon's algorithm places units, sellers can end up with stock in Poland or Czechia they never "decided" on. Storage-country lists vary by source (DE, FR, IT, ES, PL, with CZ under the Central Europe programme). The European Fulfilment Network (stock in one country, cross-border shipping) trades higher fees for fewer registrations. — [Taxually](https://www.taxually.com/blog/amazon-fba-vat-europe-guide); [hellotax: Pan-EU vs EFN](https://hellotax.com/blog/amazon-pan-eu-vat-registration-vs-efn/); [Commenda](https://www.commenda.io/blog/vat-risks-when-warehousing-in-europe); [Hayot Expertise, 2026](https://hayot-expertise.fr/en/blog/amazon-fba-vat-foreign-stock-and-filings-in-2026) (VAT-service vendors, which have an interest)
- **FBA capacity limits (mostly US evidence)**: in May 2025 Amazon reportedly moved the capacity calculation from 6 to 5 months of projected sales. Some sellers say they lost 40–75% of their space (one went from 450 to 130 ft³). Limits are in cubic feet, and in-transit and created shipments count immediately. ASIN-level restock limits are back. AWD storage does not count toward FBA limits. — [AMZ Prep](https://amzprep.com/amazon-fba-capacity-cut-seller-guide/); [Be Bold Digital](https://www.bebolddigital.com/news/amazon-fba-restock-limits-2026); [Webgility](https://webgility.com/blog/amazon-inventory-limits). **No 2026 EU capacity notice was found.** An undated item reports capacity constraints in DE, FR, IT, ES, CZ and PL FCs. — [EcommerceBytes tag](https://www.ecommercebytes.com/tag/amazon-capacity)
- **Multi-Channel Fulfilment (MCF)** is offered on amazon.es: existing FBA stock can fulfil orders from other channels, pay-as-you-go. Germany ships eligible items in neutral packaging at no extra cost. France excludes clothing, footwear and items over about 45.72×35.56×20.32 cm or 9 kg. Multi-item discounts go up to 25%. FR/ES pages show 1-day and 3–5 business-day options. Maximum compensation for a lost item was €275 as of Aug 2024. Brands without a Seller Central account can use it in DE. — [sell.amazon.es MCF](https://sell.amazon.es/logistica-de-amazon/gestion-multicanal); [sell.amazon.fr MCF](https://sell.amazon.fr/expedie-par-amazon/expedie-multicanal); [EcomCrew on MCF Germany](https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-launches-multi-channel-fulfillment-in-germany/)
- MCF fees for European parcels and oversize were raised from 7 April 2023. — [SC-EU forum announcement](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/ab96a822d74dea83b931a1f993fb3cce)
- **Overselling**: on FBM, overselling directly raises the pre-fulfilment cancellation rate (cap 2.5%), and FBA cancellations do not count against it. — [Vortex IQ KPI doc](https://docs.vortexiq.ai/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/amazon-seller/pre-fulfilment-cancel-rate)
- The root cause is non-real-time sync: channels syncing at different intervals (for example hourly vs every 15 minutes) keep selling stock that is gone. The standard mitigation is one master stock figure with per-channel buffers. — [Katana MRP](https://katanamrp.com/blog/shopify-amazon-inventory/); [Sellercloud](https://sellercloud.com/blog/how-to-eliminate-overselling-in-e-commerce/); [Stockagile](https://stockagile.com/en/integrations/amazon/) (vendor sources; Stockagile's "90% fewer cancellations" is marketing)
- **Delivery promise**: the 90% OTDR (section 1) is measured against the "Deliver by" date, so handling time and carrier transit settings in shipping templates now drive listing survival in ES. — [SP-API changelog](https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/changelog/sp-api-updates-eu-fbm-requirements-product-type-definitions-api-title-structure-external-fulfillment-api-shipment-status)

### Inferences
- **Portuguese seller archetype**: FBM from Portugal to Spanish buyers, with 1–3 day transit, plus the own PrestaShop store, Worten and KuantoKusta. That seller is most exposed to *both* overselling (shared stock across 3–4 channels) and the OTDR rule (cross-border transit), and is the strongest fit for a hub's stock and order core.
- **Spanish seller archetype**: more likely to use FBA (Spanish FCs) and sometimes Pan-EU. The hub must then treat FBA stock as a *separate, read-only location* (FBA inventory notifications) and possibly route PrestaShop orders to MCF.
- Pan-EU VAT is a pain the hub cannot solve, but it can *surface* where stock sits (FBA inventory by country) for the seller's tax adviser.

### Gaps
- No official data on the share of Iberian sellers using FBA vs FBM vs SFP, except "38% of Portuguese sellers use Amazon logistics" (2022–23 release).
- Current 2026 EU FBA capacity rules were not confirmed.
- Real handling-time and carrier-performance data for PT→ES deliveries was not found.

## 4. Fees and profitability

### Takeaway
On amazon.es the fixed cost is low (€39/month Professional plan). The margin pain comes from stacked variable fees: a category referral fee (about 6–45%), FBA fulfilment, seasonal storage (Oct–Dec storage costs about 1.7× Jan–Sep), a 1.5% fuel and logistics surcharge since April 2026, returns and liquidation fees raised for 2026, and advertising. Spanish commentary puts fees at about 20% of price before ads. Per-SKU real margin is the gap that tools like sellerboard ($15+/month) fill.

### Cited Findings
- Professional plan: **€39/month excl. VAT** plus selling fees. Amazon.es says it pays off at about 40 sales/month. The referral fee varies by category. — [sell.amazon.es/precios](https://sell.amazon.es/precios); [sell.amazon.es/aprender](https://sell.amazon.es/aprender)
- The variable closing fee applies only to media categories. — [Trendencias guide](https://www.trendencias.com/otros/como-vender-amazon-guia-basica-para-firmas-pequenas-disenadores-emergentes)
- Referral fees range 6–45% by category (third-party). The common "15% / 8% electronics" figures are **amazon.com** rates, and amazon.es differs. — [ecomcalctools: Amazon fees Spain 2026](https://ecomcalctools.com/blog/fees-amazon/amazon-fees-spain-2026/); [Repricer ES fee guide](https://www.repricer.com/es/blog/amazon-seller-fees/)
- **2026 EU fee update (Amazon official)**: Amazon cut fees by about €0.17 per unit on average across Europe. Selective increases to **monthly storage, return-to-seller and liquidation fees** make the net change about **+€0.02 per FBA unit sold**. ## 6. Returns, buyer messages, A-to-z claims, VAT/OSS and invoicing

### Takeaway
Operationally, sellers must answer buyer messages and return requests fast, and A-to-z claims are often decided against them. The bigger Iberia-specific pain is invoicing. In **Portugal**, Amazon-generated invoices are not AT-certified, so every Amazon sale must be re-invoiced in certified software. In **Spain**, Amazon's VCS documents are reported as not valid Spanish invoices and not Verifactu-compatible, and Verifactu becomes mandatory on 1 Jan 2027 (companies) or 1 Jul 2027 (others).

### Cited Findings
- Buyer messages and return requests: a **2019** SC-EU forum post says sellers must respond within **48 hours**, and claims without a seller response can be granted against the seller. Appeals have a 30-day window. — [SC-EU forum "New A-Z CLAIMS Update" (2019)](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/b0a284828d0f33d7fb7f3b766bd56638)

- Return fraud: 13.7% of online returns in the US in 2023 were fraudulent (WSJ via Genbeta). Counterfeit or used items are sent back instead of the original. **US figure.** — [Genbeta](https://www.genbeta.com/actualidad/cada-vez-estafan-a-vendedores-amazon-fraudes-ponen-peligro-modelo-devoluciones-portal)
- Return-to-seller and liquidation FBA fees rise in 2026. — [About Amazon EU](https://www.aboutamazon.eu/news/empowering-small-business/update-to-european-referral-and-fulfilment-by-amazon-fees-for-2026)

Software makers have been bound since 29 Jul 2025. Congressional validation of the decree is not confirmed in the results. — [Legal Today, 4 Dec 2025](https://www.legaltoday.com/actualidad-juridica/noticias-de-derecho/nueva-prorroga-verifactu-no-sera-obligatorio-hasta-2027-para-sociedades-y-otros-contribuyentes-2025-12-04/); [KPMG tax alert](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/es/pdf/2025/12/tax-alert-el-real-decreto-ley-15-2025-amplia-plazos-adaptacion-reglamento-verifactu.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf); [VATIT](https://vatit.com/e-invoicing-guide/spain-verifactu/). **Conflict:** older pages still give the superseded 2026 dates (RD 254/2025). — [Meridian Global Services](https://meridianglobalservices.com/verifactu-regulation-in-spain-what-businesses-need-to-know-for-2026/)

### Inferences
- **Invoicing is the single most Iberia-specific, recurring (per order) Amazon pain found.** Every Portuguese seller must re-invoice each Amazon order in AT-certified software, and Spanish sellers face the same from 2027 under Verifactu. A hub that pulls Amazon orders (with buyer tax data) and pushes them to certified invoicing software (InvoiceXpress, Moloni, Vendus or PHC in PT; Verifactu-compliant tools in ES) is a strong, sticky feature. - Buyer-message triage (with AI draft replies in ES/PT) is a plausible hub feature, but Messaging API scope was not researched.

## 7. Integration reality for a third-party tool: SP-API access, roles, PII, fees, rate limits, notifications, build cost

### Takeaway
Building an Amazon connector is feasible for a solo developer and, after the fee U-turn, free in API charges. Amazon announced a $1,400/year fee plus GET-call charges in Nov 2025 and cancelled them on 12 May 2026 "at this time". The real costs are (a) public-developer registration and review, which is harder and includes an architecture review and security assessment if you need PII for invoicing or shipping labels, (b) very tight order-listing rate limits that push you to notifications, and (c) the JSON product-type schemas for listings (flat-file and XML have been dead since July 2025).

### Cited Findings
- **Fee history**: announced **3 Nov 2025**, a **$1,400 annual subscription** from 31 Jan 2026 plus monthly GET-call-based charges from 30 Apr 2026. Delayed in January, postponed indefinitely on 9 Mar 2026, **cancelled on 12 May 2026**. Amazon "will not move forward with the SP-API usage and annual fees at this time". The fee preview dashboard is being removed and developers can delete stored cards. — [Amazon notice: cancellation of SP-API fees](https://developer.amazonservices.com/cancellation-of-sp-api-fees); [Nova Analytics](https://novadata.io/resources/news/amazon-cancels-sp-api-fees-may-2026); [Fivetran, 15 Jul 2026](https://www.fivetran.com/blog/no-walled-gardens-amazon-reversed-its-seller-partner-api-fees); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/amazon-drops-sp-api-fees-after-developer-pushback)
- **Conflict:** Amazon's Chinese-language notice describes the fees as *postponed* with a timeline "in the fall". This is likely the March 2026 stage. — [Amazon notice (zh-CN)](https://developer.amazonservices.com/sp-api-fees-update-march-2026-zh-cn). "At this time" leaves room for a future proposal.
- **Public developer registration**: a public website describing your services is required. Log-in-only, under-construction or certificate-warning sites are rejected. You choose roles in the developer profile, and Amazon reviews functionality, Acceptable Use Policy, Data Protection Policy and data security measures. You get 5 days to answer follow-up questions or the case closes. — [SP-API: Register as a Public Developer](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/lang-US/docs/register-as-a-public-developer); [Create a Developer Profile](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/onboarding-step-3-create-a-developer-profile)
- **Restricted (PII) roles**: these add an architecture review of data flows and PII controls (possibly a screen-share demo), then a data security assessment. A third-party forum post reports a denial after the assessment (anecdotal). — [SP-API: applying for PII roles](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/applying-for-pii-roles)
- PII (buyer info, shipping address) is accessed via a **Restricted Data Token** (`createRestrictedDataToken`), only with the restricted role and a genuine need such as shipping or **tax invoicing**. Restricted report types need an RDT on `getReportDocument`. Delegation to another app is possible. — [Authorization with the RDT](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/authorization-with-the-restricted-data-token); [Delegate authorization to access PII](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/delegate-authorization-to-access-pii)
- **Rate limits** (per seller-account-and-app pair): Orders v0 `getOrders` **0.0167 req/s (about 1/minute), burst 20**, and `getOrder` 0.5 req/s, burst 30. The new Orders API v2026-01-01 `searchOrders` is **0.0056 req/s (about 1 per 3 minutes), burst 20**. Usage-plan limits can differ from the `x-amzn-RateLimit-Limit` header. — [Orders API rate limits](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/lang-US/docs/orders-api-rate-limits)
- **Notifications**: `createSubscription` per notification type and payload version. Types include `LISTINGS_ITEM_STATUS_CHANGE` (EventBridge: buyable transitions, create, delete), `FBA_INVENTORY_AVAILABILITY_CHANGES` (SQS: FBA quantity snapshot across a region's marketplaces) and `ORDER_CHANGE`. Delivery is via **AWS SQS or EventBridge**, so you need an AWS account. — [Notifications API use-case guide](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/sp-api-event-driven-architecture); [Set up notifications with SQS](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/set-up-notifications-with-amazon-sqs)
- **Listings**: Listings Items API or `JSON_LISTINGS_FEED` only since 31 Jul 2025. Product Type Definitions JSON schemas allow pre-submission validation but require more (conditional) attributes. — [Deprecation notice](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/changelog/deprecation-of-feeds-api-support-for-xml-and-flat-file-listings-feeds); [Migration guide](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/listings-management-workflow-migration)
- 2026 EU FBM changes include an External Fulfillment API shipment-status update, so connectors must keep up with EU-specific changes. — [SP-API changelog](https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/changelog/sp-api-updates-eu-fbm-requirements-product-type-definitions-api-title-structure-external-fulfillment-api-shipment-status)

### Inferences
- **Build-cost estimate (inference, not sourced)** for a solo developer to reach a production-grade amazon.es connector:
  - Scope: OAuth app authorization, Orders via notifications plus throttled polling, inventory and price push via Listings Items `patch`, listing create via Product Type Definitions plus a JSON schema validator, shipment confirmation with tracking, FBA inventory read, and Finances/settlement for margin.
  - Effort: likely **several person-months** spread across about 5 subdomains.
  - Calendar time: registration and review for public and restricted roles add **weeks** of lead time that is independent of coding.
  - Running cost: low (SQS/EventBridge usage). No API fee as of Oct 2026, with the risk of re-introduction.
- Rate limits make **event-driven design mandatory**: 1 `getOrders` per minute per seller rules out "poll every 30 s" stock and order sync. This fits the repo's existing BullMQ/Redis queues (one rate-limited queue per seller-account and API operation).
- Request the **PII restricted role only if the hub does invoicing or labels**. Without it, the hub can still sync stock, prices, listings and order IDs, and buyer-facing invoicing could be delegated to the invoicing partner's own Amazon integration (InvoiceXpress/Kapta already has one).
- An alternative short-cut is to integrate Amazon *through* an aggregator (for example Base.com or ChannelEngine, if they expose APIs), at the cost of margin and control. Not researched here.

### Gaps
- No public figure on SP-API connector development cost or time was found. The estimate above is inference.
- Listings Items API rate limits and the exact `ORDER_CHANGE` payload were not retrieved.
- The Amazon Ads API registration path was not covered.
- No 2026 information on review turnaround times for public or restricted roles.

## 9. Which of these pains a multichannel SaaS hub (PrestaShop-first, PT→ES) could solve

### Takeaway
The hub can credibly own five Amazon pains, all fed by the SP-API without a fee as of Oct 2026:
1. Overselling and cancellation-rate protection, through a master stock with channel buffers, event-driven sync and FBA as a stock location.
2. FBM delivery-promise and tracking compliance for the new 90% OTDR.
3. Catalog readiness, through PIM fields for GTIN, GPSR, EPR and translations validated against Amazon's JSON schemas.
4. Per-SKU multichannel margin including Amazon fees and ad spend.
5. Order-to-certified-invoice handoff for PT (AT) and ES (Verifactu 2027).

It can only *alert* on account health, hijacking and Pan-EU VAT exposure, and should not try to replace appeals, Brand Registry enforcement or tax registration.

### Cited Findings
- Overselling on FBM raises the 2.5% cancellation metric, and batch sync intervals cause it. — [Vortex IQ](https://docs.vortexiq.ai/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/amazon-seller/pre-fulfilment-cancel-rate); [Katana MRP](https://katanamrp.com/blog/shopify-amazon-inventory/)
- The 90% FBM OTDR is enforced in ES since 15 Jul 2026, with possible listing deactivation from 1 Sep 2026. — [SP-API changelog](https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/changelog/sp-api-updates-eu-fbm-requirements-product-type-definitions-api-title-structure-external-fulfillment-api-shipment-status)
- GPSR has applied to all offers since 13 Dec 2024, and EPR numbers are required per category for non-established sellers on amazon.es. — [M2E Pro](https://blog.m2epro.com/gpsr-requirements-what-s-new-for-amazon-and-ebay-sellers-in-the-eu/); [ChannelX](https://channelx.world/2023/10/epr-requirements-in-spain-for-amazon-sellers/)

- Notifications exist for listing status, FBA inventory and orders. — [Notifications API guide](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/sp-api-event-driven-architecture)

### Inferences
Pain-to-module mapping. Frequency is how often the pain bites, severity is how badly. Both are judgement calls, not measured.

| Amazon pain | Frequency | Severity | Hub module | Feasibility / dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Overselling → cancellations (>2.5%) | Daily for FBM multichannel sellers | High (offers deactivated) | Stock/WMS master + per-channel buffers + event-driven push | High. Listings Items patch, notifications, BullMQ per-seller throttling |
| Late delivery vs promise (OTDR 90%, LSR 4%, VTR 95%) | Daily | High since 1 Sep 2026 | Orders desk: ship-by countdown, tracking upload, handling-time and template advice | High. Order and shipment APIs, carrier integrations (CTT, Correos, SEUR, etc.) |
| Suppressed listings (GPSR, EPR, missing attributes, GTIN) | Weekly | Medium–high | PIM compliance fields + JSON-schema validation + AI translation ES/PT/FR/IT/DE | Medium–high. Product Type Definitions API. Translation quality needs human review |
| Not knowing real margin per SKU | Monthly (settlement cycles) | Medium | Metrics: fees (Finances/Reports) + ads + COGS, cross-channel | Medium. Ads data needs the separate Amazon Ads API (not researched) |
| Re-invoicing each Amazon order (PT now, ES 2027) | Per order | Medium (legal risk, hours) | Connector to certified invoicing (InvoiceXpress/Moloni/Vendus; Verifactu tools) | Medium. Needs PII restricted role and security assessment, or delegation |
| Using FBA stock for PrestaShop/Worten orders | Per order (FBA users) | Medium | Order routing to MCF | Medium. Check marketplace rules on Amazon-shipped parcels (not researched) |
| Aged FBA stock, Q4 storage surge | Monthly or seasonal | Medium | Inventory ageing alerts, replenishment AI | Medium. FBA inventory reports |
| Account health deterioration | Rare but critical | Very high | Alerts on metric trends (cancellation, OTDR, ODR) | Medium. Depends on performance data via SP-API (unverified) |
| Hijacking / brand overwrite | Occasional | High | Detail-page drift alerts via LISTINGS_ITEM_STATUS_CHANGE / catalog diff | Low–medium. Detection only |
| Pan-EU VAT registrations | Ongoing (Pan-EU users) | High | Show stock by country for tax adviser | Low. Partner with VAT firms |
| Suspensions, POA, KYC | Rare | Very high | None beyond documents vault | Out of scope (agencies) |

- **Positioning implication**: the strongest Amazon story for a PT-first hub is "sell on amazon.es from Portugal without breaking your cancellation and delivery metrics, and with legal AT invoices". It combines the two daily pains with the one Portugal-specific legal pain, none of which sellerboard or Helium 10 address.
- **Sequencing implication**: start the Amazon connector with *non-PII* roles (listings, pricing, inventory, orders without addresses) to get through review faster. Add the PII restricted role once invoicing and labels are built.
