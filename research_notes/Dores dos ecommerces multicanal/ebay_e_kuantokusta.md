## 1. KuantoKusta: how it works for sellers (model, scale, fees, payouts, performance rules, catalogue)

### Takeaway
KuantoKusta is a Portuguese price comparator that added a transactional marketplace in 2017/2018. It now sells two seller plans: **DUO**, which combines a CPC redirect to the seller's own shop with a marketplace "buy here" button, and **Marketplace-only**. Its own terms set an operational regime close to Mirakl's: a **24 h order-acceptance limit** (with a conflicting 2-business-day figure elsewhere), **shipping plus tracking within 2 business days** for items shown as available, and **fines or suspension above a 3% cancellation rate**. The commission is a percentage of the **order total including shipping and VAT**. Third parties put it at 4–18% by category. Scale figures conflict widely (800 to 5,500+ sellers), and none are official.

### Cited Findings
**Business model**
- The "Registar Loja" page offers two models. **DUO** combines CPC with Marketplace: an orange button sells on the marketplace and earns KuantoKusta a commission per completed sale, while a blue button sends the shopper to the seller's own site and is billed per click. **Marketplace** is the pure-marketplace plan. KuantoKusta's pitch line is "Não somos concorrentes dos nossos Parceiros" (we do not compete with our partners). **[HARD, official page, undated]** — [KuantoKusta Registar Loja](https://www.kuantokusta.pt/public/registar-loja); [marketplace.kuantokusta.pt](https://marketplace.kuantokusta.pt/)
- The marketplace launched in November 2018. A 40% revenue rise in Q3 2018 was given as a reason for the move, and KuantoKusta said it would act only as an intermediary, not as a competing retailer. **[PRESS, 2018]** — [ECO, 14 Nov 2018](https://eco.sapo.pt/2018/11/14/comparador-de-precos-kuantokusta-estreia-se-como-marketplace/)
- **Conflict on the start date:** one Zunapro guide says the marketplace has existed "desde 2017" and another says KuantoKusta "pivotou" in 2018. **[VENDOR]** — [Zunapro melhores marketplaces](https://www.zunapro.com/portugal/pt/blog/melhores-marketplaces-portugal); [Zunapro guide](https://www.zunapro.com/portugal/pt/blog/vender-kuantokusta-guia-vendedores)
- On marketplace orders, the buyer contracts **directly with the seller**. KuantoKusta is an intermediary that **collects payment on the seller's behalf** and processes refunds on the seller's behalf. The **3-year legal warranty is the seller's responsibility**. **[HARD, marketplace T&C]** — [KuantoKusta Termos e Condições do Marketplace](https://www.kuantokusta.pt/public/termos-e-condicoes-do-marketplace); [KuantoKusta Comprar no KuantoKusta](https://www.kuantokusta.pt/public/kuantokusta-marketplace)
- Instalment financing is offered through **FLOA Bank** (terms in force from 1 Dec 2023). **[HARD]** — [FLOA terms PDF](https://www.kuantokusta.pt/documents/termos-condicoes-FLOA-Bank.pdf); [Facilidades de Pagamento](https://www.kuantokusta.pt/public/facilidades-pagamento)

**Scale (all unofficial, and conflicting)**
- About 4M+ monthly unique users, **5,500+ active sellers** and a catalogue of **15M offers** (2026 guide). **[VENDOR, Zunapro]** — [Zunapro guide](https://www.zunapro.com/portugal/pt/blog/vender-kuantokusta-guia-vendedores)

Marketplace stores get a dedicated account manager. **[PRESS, 2021]** — [Forbes Portugal](https://www.forbespt.com/?p=23115)
- Lengow's 2026 Iberia map (40+ active marketplaces) lists KuantoKusta among the Iberian-born players. Lengow calls it a price-comparison pioneer with an open marketplace that drives high-intent traffic in **tech and household** categories, and recommends it for **electronics and appliances**, alongside Worten and Fnac. **[VENDOR, Lengow]** — [Lengow Iberia 2026 map](https://blog.lengow.com/iberian-e-commerce-marketplaces-the-complete-map/); [PPC Land summary](https://ppc.land/iberias-2026-marketplace-map-where-amazon-meets-temu-and-wallapop/); [Lengow top marketplaces Portugal](https://www.lengow.com/get-to-know-more/top-marketplaces-in-portugal/)

**Fees and payouts**

Rate tables are published as separate PDFs for **KKMarketplace** and for **Lojas DUO**. **[HARD, seller general conditions]** — [KuantoKusta seller CG terms PDF](https://www.kuantokusta.pt/documents/cpc-termos.pdf); [mystore terms page](https://mystore.kuantokusta.pt/areareservada/modulos/mod_prefil_terms.php)

**Performance rules (acceptance, shipping, cancellation, penalties)**
- **Acceptance (two figures that conflict):** the marketplace T&C (clause 2.4) gives the seller **2 business days to confirm or decline** a forwarded order, while the seller terms set a **24 h maximum acceptance time**, with suspension for repeated breaches. **[HARD, but the two documents differ]** — [Marketplace T&C](https://www.kuantokusta.pt/public/termos-e-condicoes-do-marketplace); [Seller CG terms PDF](https://www.kuantokusta.pt/documents/cpc-termos.pdf)
- **Shipping:** after acceptance, the seller must ship within the period it published (clause 2.7). For items shown as immediately available, **shipping and tracking are due within 2 business days of order validation**. **[HARD]** — [Marketplace T&C](https://www.kuantokusta.pt/public/termos-e-condicoes-do-marketplace); [Seller CG terms PDF](https://www.kuantokusta.pt/documents/cpc-termos.pdf)
- **Penalties:** fines or account suspension for missing shipping deadlines, for a **cancellation rate above 3%**, and for **seller-initiated cancellations above 3%**. Fines are also tied to **response-time breaches**. Some fines are a **fixed percentage of the order value**, and repeat offences can lead to suspension or termination. **[HARD]** — [Seller CG terms PDF](https://www.kuantokusta.pt/documents/cpc-termos.pdf)
- **Offer accuracy:** the seller must keep **stock and delivery times up to date**, deliver to the **whole Portuguese territory**, and, on CPC, use **no redirects** on the product landing page. Offers must show prices and lead times the seller can actually meet. Price or shipping-cost mismatches can get the account **suspended or blocked**. **[HARD]** — [Seller CG terms PDF](https://www.kuantokusta.pt/documents/cpc-termos.pdf); [KuantoKusta Registar Loja](https://www.kuantokusta.pt/public/registar-loja)
- One consumer complaint says the shop missed a **2-business-day deadline to answer the customer**. This is not confirmed as an official SLA. **[ANECDOTE; probably this complaint, attribution within the search set uncertain]** — [Portal da Queixa (search set)](https://portaldaqueixa.com/brands/kuantokusta/complaints/kuantokusta-encomenda-atrasada-e-impossibilidade-de-contacto-66293121)

**Catalogue requirements**
- I found no official EAN or attribute specification. Feed tools describe a generic comparator feed (ID, GTIN/EAN, title, brand, image URL, product URL, price, category, availability), but not KuantoKusta's actual schema. **[VENDOR, generic; the generic field list came from one of the last two links, which one was not identified]** — [Mulwi KuantoKusta feed](https://mulwi.com/kuantokusta-feed/); [Expando XML feed requirements](https://help.expan.do/en/articles/9429726-xml-feed-requirements-how-to-connect-an-xml-feed); [100shoppers product feed XML](https://100shoppers.com/product-feed-xml/)

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE] Severity.** A 3% cancellation ceiling means a seller with **100 marketplace orders a month can afford only 3 cancellations** before fines or suspension. One oversold fast SKU that is shared with Worten, the PrestaShop shop and eBay can use up that allowance in a single day. This is the same failure mode as Worten (Mirakl), so a single stock-reservation and acceptance-guard engine covers both.
- **[INFERENCE] Frequency.** Acceptance (24 h) and the 2-business-day ship-plus-tracking rule are **daily** tasks for any active seller. The 3% cancellation and response-time rules are **monthly** risk metrics. Both suit automation: auto-accept when stock is confirmed, auto-push tracking, and SLA alerts.
- **[INFERENCE] Margin pain.** Because the commission is charged on **shipping plus VAT**, the effective rate on the net product price is higher than the headline rate. For example, a 12% commission on a €100 order including VAT (€81.30 net) takes €12, about 14.8% of the net. A per-channel margin calculator that treats commission-on-gross correctly is a concrete, PT-specific feature.
- **[INFERENCE]** DUO sellers face a **two-cost-model decision for each product**: pay CPC on the blue button, or commission on the orange one. Choosing between them needs click-to-sale data from the seller's own site plus marketplace margins. That is a natural "metrics + AI decision support" use case that no global tool would build for one Portuguese channel.
- **[INFERENCE]** The seller counts conflict (800 vs 900+ vs 5,500+). They probably measure different things: 5,500+ could include CPC-only shops, and 800 to 900 could be marketplace sellers. This is unverified. For a TAM estimate, the conservative figure is **about 800 to 900 marketplace sellers**.

### Gaps
- I found no official current commission table (the KKMarketplace and DUO PDFs are referenced but did not surface), and I could not confirm whether the €29.90/month fee still applies.
- I found no payout frequency (weekly, fortnightly or monthly) and no reserve or chargeback rules for sellers.
- I could not resolve whether acceptance is limited to 24 h or 2 business days, or which document is in force.
- I found no official number of categories, no EAN-mandatory rule and no attribute schema.
- I found no official seller count, GMV or 2025/2026 traffic figure.

## 2. KuantoKusta: how sellers integrate (feeds, Seller API, connectors)

### Takeaway
KuantoKusta has a real **Seller API** (KK Seller API v1.2.0, OpenAPI 3.0, Swagger UI at `seller.kuantokusta.pt/api/kms/`) with groups for **orders, offers, offer management, cancellation reasons and couriers**. Its rate limits are **tight: 5 requests/s, 20/10 s, 30/min per API key**, and exceeding them blocks the key for 30 s. Connector coverage is thin. Zunapro (a Portuguese agency) claims a full connector, and Contentserv (an enterprise PIM) and Mulwi (feeds) list KuantoKusta. I found no evidence that **Koongo, Lengow, Channable or Base.com** sync KuantoKusta **orders**. This is a genuine local integration gap.

### Cited Findings
- **Official API:** the Swagger UI describes the "KK Seller API", **version 1.2.0, OpenAPI 3.0**, with groups for **orders, offers, cancellation-reasons, couriers and offer-management**. **[HARD]** — [KuantoKusta Seller API Swagger](https://seller.kuantokusta.pt/api/kms/)
- **Rate limits, per API key:** **5 requests per 1 s, 20 per 10 s and 30 per 60 s**. Going over returns **HTTP 429 (code KMS0429)** with a `Retry-After` header and **blocks the key for 30 s**. **[HARD]** — [KuantoKusta Seller API Swagger](https://seller.kuantokusta.pt/api/kms/)
- **Authentication:** an API key. A community **Node.js SDK** shows API-key auth and a sample "get all orders" call, and points to the official docs for details. **[ANECDOTE / community code, not verified against the spec]** — [GitHub bsolus/kuantokusta-seller-api-node-sdk](https://github.com/bsolus/kuantokusta-seller-api-node-sdk)
- A third-party project reads offers via **`GET /v2/kms/offers`**. That project notes it called the real API only once, with an invalid key, so the path or version may differ from the spec. **[ANECDOTE, code]** — [GitHub pharma-hub PR #8](https://github.com/mariliacamara/pharma-hub/pull/8); [PR #9](https://github.com/mariliacamara/pharma-hub/pull/9)
- Community experiments also exist, such as an MCP server for KuantoKusta and price-comparison browser extensions that scrape public pages rather than use the API. **[ANECDOTE]** — [GitHub kuantokusta-mcp](https://github.com/ThiagoJacobLannes/kuantokusta-mcp); [GitHub Kombina](https://github.com/pedrobarbosak/Kombina); [GitHub Kuantokusta org](https://github.com/Kuantokusta)
- A merchant with **only the Swagger link and no integration guide** was told on the Shopify Community that automatic order creation needs webhooks and a developer (found in the earlier research round). **[ANECDOTE]** — [Shopify Community thread](https://community.shopify.com/t/install-markeplace-api-on-shopify-to-automatically-create-orders/62729)
- **Zunapro** advertises a KuantoKusta connector that links **Shopify, WooCommerce, PrestaShop, Magento, Jumpseller or an ERP "em 10 minutos"** and automates **e-Fatura and SAF-T PT**. **[VENDOR]** — [Zunapro KuantoKusta page](https://www.zunapro.com/portugal/pt/mercado/kuantokusta)
- **Contentserv** (an enterprise PIM) lists a "KuantoKusta connector" in its marketplace. Scope not verified (probably product data). **[VENDOR]** — [Contentserv Marketplace](https://marketplace.contentserv.com/connectors/kuantokusta-connector)
- **Mulwi** sells a product-feed generator "to sell your merchandise on KuantoKusta". It covers feeds only. **[VENDOR]** — [Mulwi](https://mulwi.com/kuantokusta-feed/)
- **Koongo:** its pages name Amazon, Bol, Cdiscount, Kaufland, eBay, Zalando and others. Its order-sync list (Amazon, Azoya, Bol.com, Beslist.nl, eBay, idealo, Wish, Spartoo, Zalando, Miinto) **does not include KuantoKusta**. For channels without a native connector, Koongo offers feed files via API, FTP or upload. **[VENDOR]** — [Koongo FAQ](https://www.koongo.com/faq/); [Koongo Shopify app](https://apps.shopify.com/koongo); [Koongo help](https://intercom.help/koongo-607303707ce4/en/articles/8582722)
- **Lengow:** it claims 1,800+ channel integrations and sells order management as a **paid add-on that is "not for all marketplaces"**. No KuantoKusta order connector surfaced, although Lengow maps KuantoKusta in its Iberia content. **[VENDOR]** — [Lengow](https://lengow.com/?p=61949); [Capterra ES Lengow vs Channable](https://www.capterra.es/compare/162794/182568/lengow/vs/channable); [Lengow Iberia map](https://blog.lengow.com/iberian-e-commerce-marketplaces-the-complete-map/)
- **Channable** (2,500+ channels claimed, 23M orders processed in 2025) and **Base.com/BaseLinker** (2,000+ integrations, about 20 marketplaces with order intake): **no KuantoKusta integration surfaced** in two searches. **[VENDOR, absence of evidence]** — [Capterra ES](https://www.capterra.es/compare/162794/182568/lengow/vs/channable); [Base.com B&Q integration (example of a two-way marketplace connector)](https://base.com/en-GB/integrations/bandquk/blconnect); [BaseLinker order manager](https://help.baselinker.com/knowledgebase/order-manager-how-to-use/)

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE] The rate limit shapes the design.** At 30 calls a minute, per-SKU stock pushes are impossible for any real catalogue: 2,000 SKUs would take more than an hour, which breaks the "keep stock up to date" obligation. A connector needs **batched or bulk offer updates where the API allows them, delta-only pushes (changed SKUs only), a token-bucket queue per tenant API key, and 429/Retry-After handling**. The BullMQ queue already in this stack fits this (per-tenant limiter on a `kuantokusta` queue). Because the limit is **per key**, a multi-tenant hub is not throttled across tenants, but each tenant's sync must budget its own 30/min for orders, offers and tracking.
- **[INFERENCE] Moat.** No global tool (Koongo, Lengow, Channable, Base) visibly syncs KuantoKusta orders, and the only full connector found is a local agency (Zunapro) that bundles e-Fatura/SAF-T. A PrestaShop-first hub with **native KuantoKusta orders + stock + tracking + PT invoicing hand-off** would fill a gap that foreign tools are unlikely to close, given a market of roughly 800 to 900 marketplace sellers.
- **[INFERENCE] Build cost.** The API surface looks small (orders, offers, couriers, cancellation reasons), and auth is an API key with no OAuth. A solo developer could probably build an orders-and-stock connector in about **1–3 weeks**. The hard parts are the rate-limit queue and order-state mapping (accept/refuse, cancel with reason code, ship with courier and tracking). This is an estimate, not sourced.
- **[INFERENCE]** The DUO CPC side runs on a **product feed** (XML/CSV) and the marketplace side on the **API**. A seller on DUO therefore maintains two integration paths for the same catalogue. A hub can generate the feed from the PIM and drive the API from the same SKU records.

### Gaps
- I could not read the endpoint list, payload schemas, order-state machine, webhook support (none surfaced, so polling is likely, but this is unverified), or whether bulk offer updates exist.
- I found no API terms of use for third-party integrators, so I cannot say whether a SaaS may hold keys on behalf of many sellers.
- I found no official feed specification (XML/CSV fields, EAN rules) for the CPC/DUO side.
- I could not confirm the coverage of the Zunapro and Contentserv connectors (orders vs feeds only) or their prices.

## 3. KuantoKusta: seller and buyer complaints (Portal da Queixa, forums, agency blogs)

### Takeaway
Online complaint material is **almost entirely buyer-side** (Portal da Queixa). It shows the failure modes that become **seller penalties**: shops cancelling after payment, refunds not made, late or missing deliveries, unresponsive shops, and warranty disputes. KuantoKusta replies on most complaints, holds funds until a case closes, and draws a sharp line between **marketplace orders** (it intervenes) and **CPC/direct orders** (the shop is responsible). I found **no seller-written complaints** (Reddit, Facebook groups, forums) about KuantoKusta fees or rules.

### Cited Findings
- Portal da Queixa's summary for the brand: the most frequent complaints concern **returns and refunds not made, delivery failures, and damaged or defective products**. Contact: apoiocliente@kk.pt. ### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE]** Every complaint pattern above maps to a seller-side KPI in Section 1: cancellation after payment (cancellation rate, often caused by overselling), unanswered messages (response-time fines), and late or missing delivery (shipping deadline and tracking). For the seller, the pain is less the complaint itself than the **fine, suspension and blocked funds** that follow.
- **[INFERENCE]** A **unified buyer-message inbox with SLA timers** (KuantoKusta messages next to eBay and Worten messages) is a cheap, visible win, because response time is penalised on KuantoKusta and drives defect metrics on eBay.
- **[INFERENCE]** Without seller-written sources, the seller-side pains have to come from the **terms**, not from testimony. ## 4. eBay: performance standards, seller levels, fees in Spain, managed payments and payouts

### Takeaway
eBay.es grades sellers monthly (on the 20th) into **Excelente (Top Rated), Destacado/Notable (Above Standard) and Regular (Below Standard)**. The key limits are a **2% transaction defect rate** (0.5% for Top Rated), **0.3% or 2 cases closed without seller resolution**, and late-shipment limits (European figures via a regional page: 3% for Top Rated). Below Standard can bring **extra final-value fees**, lower visibility and restrictions. Spanish business fees are **9% up to €990 per item plus 2% above**, with category exceptions, plus **€0.35/€0.45 per order**, plus a **0.35% regulatory operating fee** (since April 2024). Store subscriptions are **€19.50, €39.50 or €149.50 a month**. Managed payments makes funds available **within about 1 day**, except for holds (up to 14 days for new sellers). A **July 2026 European fee reform** (5% flat for used and refurbished in some categories, higher rates for new items) is confirmed for Germany. Whether it applies to Spain is unconfirmed.

## 5. eBay: listing pains (item specifics, EAN/MPN, catalogue matching, variations, parts compatibility, international visibility, returns)

### Takeaway
The main listing friction is **category-specific item specifics**. Missing **required aspects (most often Brand, MPN, EAN)** is a **blocking error**. These must be fetched per category from the **Taxonomy API** and mapped from the shop's attributes. Auto parts add **K-Type vehicle compatibility** (supported on eBay.es). Returns are seller-defined on eBay.es but bounded by EU law (14-day minimum) and eBay's Money Back Guarantee. The UK now forces **30-day free returns for parts and accessories** (from June 2026), a sign of where the EU sites may go.

### Cited Findings
- A missing **required item specific** returns a **blocking error** on eBay, and most required specifics are **EAN, Brand and MPN**. If no valid EAN exists, the fix is to fill in a valid EAN or use eBay's "does not apply" option. **[VENDOR, PrestaShop/eBay module docs]** — [Prestalia help](https://help.prestalia.it/en/?p=4161)
- In the Sell Inventory API, **Brand is conditionally required if the category requires an MPN**. In the Trading API, **Brand and MPN are both required** when the category or product requires an MPN. When an item has no MPN, the seller must send a marketplace-specific "not available" string. **[HARD]** — [eBay Inventory API Product type](https://developer.ebay.com/api-docs/sell/inventory/types/slr:Product); [eBay Trading API ProductListingDetailsType](https://developer.ebay.com/devzone/xml/docs/Reference/eBay/types/ProductListingDetailsType.html)
- Whether each aspect is **required, recommended or optional** in a category is exposed by the **Taxonomy API `getItemAspectsForCategory`**. Brand/MPN placed only in `ProductListingDetails` is ignored unless the pair **matches an eBay catalogue product**, so they belong in item specifics. **[HARD]** — [eBay Trading API ProductListingDetailsType](https://developer.ebay.com/devzone/xml/docs/Reference/eBay/types/ProductListingDetailsType.html)
- eBay **enforces product identifiers (GTIN/EAN/UPC/MPN)** and uses them for catalogue matching and search visibility. **[VENDOR]** — [Webinterpret](https://webinterpret.com/en/blog/why-are-ebay-required-product-identifiers-gtin-upc-ean-mpn-important)
- **Parts compatibility:** the **eBay DE, UK, FR, IT, ES and AU sites support K-Type numbers** (Name = "KType", Value = number). The kType database is **shared across sites**. kTypes express model years as a production range, while ePIDs use a fixed year. Tools (3Dsellers, M2E Pro) offer bulk K-Type CSV editing and copying compatibility from another listing. **[HARD + VENDOR]** — [eBay ItemCompatibilityType](https://developer.ebay.com/DevZone/XML/docs/Reference/eBay/types/ItemCompatibilityType.html); [M2E Pro parts compatibility](https://docs-m1.m2epro.com/parts-compatibility); [3Dsellers K-Type CSV](https://help.3dsellers.com/en/articles/7212153-ebay-csv-car-compatibility)
- **Multi-variation listings in the Inventory API** need an **Inventory Item Group**. Every item and variation needs a **unique SKU**. Price lives on the **Offer**, not the inventory item. (Details in Section 7.) **[HARD + VENDOR]** — [eBay migrating listings](https://www.developer.ebay.com/api-docs/sell/static/inventory/migrating-listings.html); [EcartAPI eBay variants](https://docs.ecartapi.com/docs/ebay-products-and-variants)
- **Returns on eBay.es:** sellers set their own return policy, and if they offer a 30-day window they must honour it. Who pays return postage depends on the policy, unless free returns are offered. **Damaged, defective or not-as-described items must be accepted even when the policy says "no returns"**, and the seller pays return shipping. **[VENDOR]** — [Edesk ES devoluciones](https://www.edesk.com/es/blog/como-gestionar-las-devoluciones-y-las-disputas-en-ebay/)
- Under consumer law, professional sellers that accept returns must allow **at least 14 calendar days**. **[Consumer association]** — [OCU](https://www.ocu.org/reclamar/lista-reclamaciones-publicas/politica-fraudulenta-devolucio/CPTES00620987-90). eBay's buyer guarantee covers non-arrival and items not as described on almost all purchases. **[HARD]** — [eBay ES garantía al cliente](https://pages.ebay.com/es/comprar/garantia-cliente)
- **UK precedent:** "Easy & Free Returns" from **8 June 2026** for **business sellers of parts and accessories**. It requires a **free return window of 30 or 60 days** for change-of-mind returns, and eBay covers **half the return label**. **[VENDOR, M2E Pro blog]** — [M2E Pro blog (ES)](https://blog.m2epro.com/es/ebay-reino-unido-exige-devoluciones-gratuitas-en-un-plazo-de-30-d-as-para-piezas-y-accesorios-lo-que-deben-hacer-los-vendedores/). eBay's Q2 2026 filing also lists UK Easy and Free Returns among motors P&A initiatives. **[HARD]** — [eBay 10-Q Q2 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001065088/000106508826000177/ebay-20260630.htm)
- **International visibility:** eBay offers store subscriptions on its international sites through its export hub. **[HARD, page not read]** — [export.ebay.com stores on international sites](https://export.ebay.com/es/marketing/ebay-store-on-international-ebay-sites-fees-and-benefits)

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE] Frequency and severity.** Item-specifics mapping is a **one-off but large** effort per category, repeated whenever eBay changes required aspects. It blocks listing outright (high severity, low frequency). For a PrestaShop seller with features and attributes in Portuguese or Spanish, mapping them to eBay aspects (in Spanish on eBay.es, German on eBay.de) is the main onboarding pain. **PIM + AI attribute mapping** fits it directly: fetch `getItemAspectsForCategory`, suggest values from title, description and features, and validate before publishing.
- **[INFERENCE]** The **EAN-first** catalogues already needed for Worten and KuantoKusta make eBay easier: the same GTIN drives catalogue matching. A hub that enforces a valid GTIN, or an explicit "no aplica", at the PIM level serves all three channels.
- **[INFERENCE]** **Auto parts** is the eBay niche where Iberian SMBs plausibly matter (eBay.es supports K-Type, and motors P&A is an eBay focus category). It is also the niche that needs specialised data (K-Type and TecDoc-style fitment). That is probably a later module, not an MVP feature.
- **[INFERENCE]** Cross-border selling on eBay (eBay.de, .fr, .it) needs **translated titles and item specifics** in each site's language. AI translation tied to the PIM is a real differentiator for PT sellers, whose home market is small.

### Gaps
- I found no eBay.es-specific statement on mandatory EAN by category, and no dated 2025/2026 enforcement change.
- I found no Spain or Portugal data on how many sellers use multi-variation listings or how often variation sync breaks.
- I found no official eBay.es page requiring 30-day free returns for any category, and no confirmation that UK-style rules are planned for ES.
- I could not research international-visibility mechanics (eBay International Shipping or Global Shipping availability for ES/PT sellers, cross-site listing visibility).

That probably mixes in FX or is a different measure, so it conflicts with the 2% organic figure. **[PRESS, headline only]** — [EcommerceBytes](https://www.ecommercebytes.com/d9tt)
- **EU low-value parcel duty:** a **€3 customs duty per item on parcels under €150** from July 2026, seen by one analyst as a headwind to eBay's cross-border GMV. **[VENDOR/analyst]** — [Simply Wall St](https://simplywall.st/stocks/us/retail/nasdaq-ebay/ebay/news/the-bull-case-for-ebay-ebay-could-change-following-eu-3-low)

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE]** For a PT-first hub, **eBay is not an MVP channel**. KuantoKusta and Worten matter more domestically. eBay becomes relevant for (a) **auto parts** sellers (K-Type on eBay.es, motors as a focus category), (b) **refurbished and used electronics**, especially if the 5% German rate spreads, (c) **collectibles and trading cards**, and (d) PT/ES sellers **exporting to eBay.de, .fr and .it**, where eBay is much larger than in Iberia (Germany and the UK are eBay's main European markets per its filings).
- **[INFERENCE]** The EU €3 duty on low-value non-EU parcels may slightly **favour EU-based (PT/ES) sellers** over Chinese, UK and US sellers on eBay's EU sites in cheap categories (parts, accessories). This is a modest tailwind for Iberian exporters, unproven.
- **[INFERENCE]** eBay's push into C2C and recommerce (Depop, used-item fee cuts) means its product roadmap is aimed less at the B2C new-goods SMB, the hub's core user. That supports treating eBay as an "add-on channel" priced into a higher tier.

## 7. eBay: APIs for third parties (Sell APIs, notifications, program terms and limits) and what an eBay connector costs a solo developer

### Takeaway
eBay's RESTful **Sell APIs** (Inventory, Fulfillment, Account) plus **Taxonomy**, the **Notification API** and the legacy **Trading API** cover everything a hub needs. Default limits suit SMB volumes (for example, **100,000 Fulfillment Order calls a day**), and a **free Application Growth Check** raises them. The non-obvious costs are compliance and legacy interop. **Marketplace Account Deletion** notifications are **mandatory** (signed with ECDSA; endpoint challenge; keysets disabled for non-compliance; must be set up before production keys are issued). **Inventory API vs Trading API listings do not interoperate**: migration is one-way, needs business policies and unique SKUs, and breaks Trading-based tools. A solo developer should budget **weeks, not days**, mostly for listing and aspect mapping, the migration path and compliance.

### Cited Findings
- **Default call limits** are set "for individuals and smaller businesses", and many applications fit within them. **Fulfillment API Order resource (all methods): 100,000 calls/day**. `getPaymentDispute` and `getPaymentDisputeSummaries`: **250,000/day** each. **[HARD]** — [eBay API call limits](https://developer.ebay.com/develop/get-started/api-call-limits); [Sell API call limits](https://developer.ebay.com/develop/api/sell/api_call_limits)
- **Raising limits:** the **Application Growth Check** is required to raise limits, is **free**, and reviews the application for **API License Agreement compliance and efficient call usage** (it limits data volume and avoids unnecessary polling). **[HARD]** — [eBay Application Growth Check](https://developer.ebay.com/api-docs/static/gs_request-an-application-growth.html)
- **Monitoring:** the Developer Analytics API `getRateLimits` and `getUserRateLimits` methods return quota, remaining calls and reset time per resource. A forum reply says a **5,000/day** limit (for some legacy calls) applies to the **application as a whole, not per user**. **[HARD + ANECDOTE]** — [getRateLimits](https://developer.ebay.com/api-docs/developer/analytics/resources/rate_limit/methods/getRateLimits); [eBay community](https://community.ebay.com/t5/eBay-APIs-Talk-to-your-fellow/About-API-limit-calls/m-p/34673055)
- **Mandatory account-deletion compliance:** every third-party developer must **subscribe to Marketplace Account Deletion notifications or opt out** (opt-out only if no eBay user data is stored). Wrong information can bring penalties or a **disabled account**. **[HARD, eBay developer support reply]** — [eBay community (developer support)](https://community.ebay.com/t5/APIs-Feedback-Comments-and/How-to-comply-with-marketplace-deletion-account-closure/m-p/34990446/highlight/true); [eBay developer newsletter Q1 2021](https://developer.ebay.com/updates/newsletter/q1_2021_news)
- **Mechanics:** endpoint verification by a **challenge-code hash**. Each notification carries an **`x-ebay-signature` (ECDSA)** verified with a public key fetched by key ID. Official Node.js and Java SDKs handle this. Missing acknowledgements (**2xx within 24 h**) can get the **keyset disabled**. **[VENDOR guide + HARD]** — [Hookdeck eBay webhooks guide](https://hookdeck.com/webhooks/platforms/guide-to-ebay-webhooks-features-and-best-practices); [eBay Notification API overview](https://www.developer.ebay.com/api-docs/commerce/notification/overview.html)
- Developers report **new keysets disabled as "Non Compliant"** and requests for exemptions. One reports that the deletion endpoint must be set up **before production App ID, Cert ID and Dev ID are issued**, and another that "Verify Endpoint" sent no request. **[ANECDOTE]** — [eBay community: keyset disabled](https://community.ebay.com/forum/ebay-developers-program-57950/topic/new-keyset-disabled-as-non-compliant-requesting-exemption-474102/); [eBay community: deletion notifications support](https://community.ebay.com/t5/eBay-APIs-Talk-to-your-fellow/Account-Deletion-Notifications-API-Support/m-p/35098661)
- **Inventory API model:** listings consist of **Inventory Location + Inventory Item + Offer** (+ **Inventory Item Group** for variations). `bulkMigrateListings` converts existing listings. Migration requires **business policies** (legacy shipping, payment and return fields block it) and a **unique SKU on every item and variation**. **[HARD]** — [eBay migrating listings](https://www.developer.ebay.com/api-docs/sell/static/inventory/migrating-listings.html); [Inventory API overview](https://developer.ebay.com/api-docs/sell/inventory/overview.html)
- **Interop pain:** once migrated, Trading-based tools get **"This operation is not allowed for inventory items"**. "eBay unfortunately didn't make the Inventory APIs compatible with the trading APIs." A Seller Hub file upload can **revert** the migration. A price sent without an Offer returns success but **changes nothing**. **Zero-stock variants are silently excluded** from migration, and migration is **one-time** (a retry errors). **[VENDOR support docs]** — [SumTracker](https://help.sumtracker.com/ebay-inventory-not-syncing-due-to-migration-of-listings-to-ebay-inventory-api); [SellerActive](https://selleractive.com/support/ebay-error-inventoryapi-based-listing-management-not-supported); [EcartAPI](https://docs.ecartapi.com/docs/ebay-products-and-variants)
- **Aspect discovery:** Taxonomy API `getItemAspectsForCategory` (see Section 5). Brand/MPN catalogue matching. **[HARD]** — [eBay Trading API ProductListingDetailsType](https://developer.ebay.com/devzone/xml/docs/Reference/eBay/types/ProductListingDetailsType.html)

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE] Connector scope and effort for a solo developer** (estimate, not sourced). Components:
  1. OAuth consent and token refresh per tenant, plus the account-deletion endpoint: about 3–5 days.
  2. Account API business policies (fetch or create return, payment and shipping policies): 2–3 days.
  3. Taxonomy category suggestion + aspect fetch + PIM mapping UI: 1–2 weeks. This is the largest piece.
  4. Inventory API item/offer/group publish + stock/price delta updates: 1–2 weeks.
  5. Fulfillment API order polling or notifications, shipping fulfilment with tracking, cancellations and returns: 1–2 weeks.
  6. Edge cases (migrated vs legacy listings, multi-site eBay.es/.de/.fr, K-Type): open-ended.

  Total for a usable MVP: **about 5–8 weeks**. API access itself costs nothing (the Growth Check is free), but there is **compliance risk**: a disabled keyset takes down every tenant at once.
- **[INFERENCE]** **Existing sellers already have Trading-API listings** (created in Seller Hub or by older tools). A hub that only speaks the Inventory API must either migrate them (one-way, with the risk of dropping zero-stock variants) or also implement the Trading API for revise and stock calls. Supporting **"adopt existing listings without migrating"** (Trading API ReviseInventoryStatus-style stock updates) is probably necessary for onboarding. This is inferred from the interop complaints, and the specific Trading call was not verified in this session.
- **[INFERENCE]** Compared with KuantoKusta (API key, 30 calls/min), eBay has a much larger API surface but far looser rate limits (100k order calls/day). The KuantoKusta connector is quick to build and rate-limit-bound. The eBay connector is slow to build, compliance-bound and mapping-bound.
- **[INFERENCE]** The hub's existing stack (NestJS + BullMQ + Mongo audit) fits eBay's needs: a public signed-notification endpoint (account deletion + order events), per-tenant token storage (encrypted, using the existing AES field crypto), and a queue with per-resource quota tracking via `getRateLimits`.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm the Sell Inventory API's own daily limits, or the per-user vs per-app semantics for REST APIs.
- I could not read the current API License Agreement terms that matter to a SaaS (data retention, caching, restrictions on price-comparison or cross-channel use, display requirements).
- I could not confirm which Notification API topics exist for orders, fulfilment and returns (versus legacy Platform Notifications), or their latency and reliability.
- I found no sourced cost estimate for building an eBay connector. The effort figures above are my own estimate.

## 8. eBay: tools sellers use and complaints in reviews

### Takeaway
Reviews of eBay multichannel tools repeat one complaint above all: **inventory sync that fails to delist or decrement in time**, leading to **overselling and cancelled eBay orders** (which feed eBay's defect metrics). The second is **variation handling**. Some tools even removed eBay variant updates, blaming eBay's API. Specialist eBay tools (M2E Pro, 3Dsellers, Codisto/Shopify Marketplace Connect) are common, and the review samples are small and mixed.

### Cited Findings
- **Nembol (Trustpilot):** one reviewer says eBay and Etsy sync was "very bad, especially with variation products", and that support blamed the user. - **[INFERENCE]** The overselling complaints are about **polling-based sync with no reservation**. On eBay, an oversell becomes a seller-cancelled transaction (a defect). On KuantoKusta it counts toward the 3% cancellation ceiling. - **[INFERENCE]** Variation sync is where eBay-specific tools fail. A hub should model **parent/child SKUs in the PIM** and map them to eBay Inventory Item Groups from day one, rather than retrofit them.
- **[INFERENCE]** None of the reviewed tools is Iberian, and none covers KuantoKusta. A PT seller on PrestaShop + KuantoKusta + Worten + eBay would today need a global tool for eBay and Worten plus a local agency (Zunapro) or custom code for KuantoKusta. That fragmentation is the hub's opening.

## 9. Which of these pains a multichannel SaaS hub could solve (cross-channel synthesis)

### Takeaway
On both channels the pains that are **daily, penalised and automatable** are the same: order acceptance and shipping/tracking deadlines, cancellations caused by overselling, response-time SLAs, and per-channel fee-aware margins. KuantoKusta adds a **local integration gap**: a small API with tight rate limits and no global connector. eBay adds a **catalogue-mapping burden** (item specifics, EAN/MPN, variations, K-Type) and **API compliance** overhead. A hub that starts from PrestaShop + KuantoKusta (local moat) and adds eBay as an export channel, using the same stock ledger and PIM, addresses most of them.

### Cited Findings
- KuantoKusta penalises **cancellations above 3%**, slow responses and missed shipping deadlines, and requires **up-to-date stock and lead times**. **[HARD]** — [KuantoKusta seller CG terms PDF](https://www.kuantokusta.pt/documents/cpc-termos.pdf)
- eBay.es limits **cases closed without seller resolution to 2 or 0.3%**, may charge **extra fees** below standard, and restricts accounts and holds payouts for **missing tracking**. **[HARD]** — [eBay.es política de rendimiento](https://www.es.ebay.com/help/policies/selling-policies/seller-performance-policy?id=4347); [eBay.es estado de la cuenta](https://www.es.ebay.com/help/account/account-account-status/wheres-account?id=4190)
- Multichannel tool reviews point to **sync failures and overselling** as the top eBay complaint. **[ANECDOTE]** — [eBay community](https://community.ebay.com/forum/seller-tools-57919/topic/multi-channel-lister-exportyourstore-442132/); [LitCommerce reviews](https://apps.shopify.com/litcommerce/reviews)
- KuantoKusta's API allows **30 calls/min per key**. **[HARD]** — [KuantoKusta Seller API Swagger](https://seller.kuantokusta.pt/api/kms/). eBay allows **100,000 Order calls/day** by default. **[HARD]** — [eBay API call limits](https://developer.ebay.com/develop/get-started/api-call-limits)

### Inferences
**[INFERENCE] Pain-to-module map (frequency and severity are my estimates for a 50–500 orders/month PT or ES SMB):**

| Pain | Channel | Frequency | Severity | Hub module that solves it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accept order within 24 h (or 2 business days); refuse with a reason when stock is missing | KK | Daily | High (suspension) | **Orders**: auto-accept when stock is reserved, SLA timers, alerting |
| Oversell → cancellation (KK 3% ceiling; eBay defect; Worten KPI) | KK, eBay | Weekly (fast SKUs) | Very high | **Stock/WMS**: central ledger, per-channel reservation, event-driven decrement, safety buffer per channel |
| Ship plus tracking within 2 business days (KK); valid tracking (eBay) | KK, eBay | Daily | High (fines, payout holds) | **Orders/shipping**: carrier label → tracking pushed to the channel automatically |
| Buyer messages and response-time fines (KK); INR/SNAD cases (eBay) | KK, eBay | Daily | Medium–high | **Unified inbox** with SLA timers and AI-drafted replies (PT/ES) |
| Commission on gross incl. shipping and VAT (KK); 9% + €0.35/0.45 + 0.35% + store fee (eBay.es) | KK, eBay | Every sale | Medium (margin leak) | **Metrics**: per-order, per-channel net margin; fee-rule engine per channel |
| DUO choice: CPC vs marketplace commission per product | KK | Monthly | Medium | **Ads + AI decision support**: CPC cost vs commission per SKU |
| Item specifics, EAN/Brand/MPN blocking errors; category aspects | eBay | Per new category / per eBay change | High (cannot list) | **PIM**: Taxonomy-driven attribute mapping, AI value suggestion, pre-publish validation |
| Variations (Item Groups, unique SKUs) | eBay | Per listing | Medium–high | **PIM**: parent/child SKU model mapped to eBay groups |
| K-Type parts compatibility | eBay (parts) | Per listing | High in the niche | Later vertical module (fitment data import) |
| Cross-border titles and specifics in DE/FR/IT | eBay | Per listing | Medium | **PIM + AI translation** |
| Stock and offer sync within KK's 30 req/min | KK | Continuous | Hidden (stale stock → penalties) | **Connector**: delta sync, per-tenant token bucket, 429/Retry-After handling (BullMQ limiter) |
| Payout holds and cash-flow visibility (KK holds until a case closes; eBay holds up to 14 days for new sellers) | KK, eBay | Monthly | Medium | **Metrics**: payout reconciliation per channel |
| PT compliance: e-Fatura / SAF-T for marketplace orders | KK | Every sale | High (legal) | Hand-off to a certified invoicing tool (Moloni, InvoiceXpress), not built in-house |

- **[INFERENCE] Sequencing.** (1) KuantoKusta orders + stock + tracking connector (small API, no global competitor, PT moat). (2) Central stock ledger and acceptance/SLA guard shared with Worten/Mirakl. (3) Fee-aware margin metrics. (4) eBay as a second-wave "export" connector, starting with the Inventory API for new listings plus Trading-API stock updates for adopted listings, and the account-deletion compliance endpoint from day one. (5) Vertical add-ons (K-Type, refurbished grading) only if early customers are in those niches.
- **[INFERENCE] Pricing angle.** eBay's store fees (€19.50–€149.50/month) and KuantoKusta's possible €29.90/month set the seller's frame for "monthly channel cost". A hub tier that bundles KuantoKusta + one more channel at about the price of one eBay Básica or Avanzada store is easy to justify. eBay could sit in a higher tier, given its lower Iberian relevance and higher build and maintenance cost.

### Gaps
- None of the frequency and severity ratings come from measured seller data. They need validation in customer-discovery interviews, for example: how many KK or eBay cancellations a month, how many hours a week on listings and messages.
- I could not confirm whether KuantoKusta's API terms allow a multi-tenant SaaS to hold seller keys, or eBay's licence constraints on cross-channel data use. Both should be checked before committing engineering time.
