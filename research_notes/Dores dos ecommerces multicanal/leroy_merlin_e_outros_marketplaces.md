# Leroy Merlin marketplace (PT/ES) and other Iberian marketplaces (excl. Worten, Amazon, eBay, KuantoKusta): seller rules, SMB pains, and what a multichannel hub can solve

> Method note (October 2026): WebFetch failed with a DNS error (`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND www.leroymerlin.pt`) on the first attempt, so every finding below comes from web-search result summaries (35 searches), not from full page reads. Snippets can lose context, so check the load-bearing numbers against the primary page before using them in a pitch. Most seller-side detail comes from integrators (Lengow, ChannelEngine, Channable, e-tailize, BigBuy, eDesk, Zunapro). They sell tools, so they have a commercial interest. Leroy Merlin does not publish its seller KPI thresholds, commission grid or content guide; these live inside the Mirakl back office that sellers see after signing up.

## 1. Leroy Merlin marketplace PT/ES: platform, scale, fees, KPIs, catalog, logistics, omnichannel, payment terms

### Takeaway
Leroy Merlin (ADEO group) runs one Mirakl-based marketplace, operated by ADEO Marketplace Services, with separate country channels for France, Italy, Spain and Portugal. Spain is the fast-growing Iberian leg: about 150 sellers at launch (Nov 2022), 1,600 sellers and €96M GMV in 2024, and about €150M in 2025. Sellers pay €39/month excl. VAT plus an unpublished commission that varies by category (and includes shipping). Entry is selective: registered home/garden/DIY company, EU address, VAT number and stock, EAN-13 on every product, and a registered trademark. Sellers handle all logistics, customer service and returns. In France, marketplace items cannot be picked up or returned in store. I found no published KPI thresholds or payout cycle.

### Cited Findings

**Platform and history**
- Leroy Merlin's marketplaces run on Mirakl: Brazil launched in November 2018 with about 40 sellers, less than five months after the project started — [Mirakl customer story, Leroy Merlin Brazil](https://www.mirakl.com/content/customer-stories/leroy-merlin-brazil/). The European (France) marketplace was announced in December 2020 "in collaboration with Mirakl" — [Mirakl news](https://mirakl.com/news/leroy-merlin-launches-its-european-marketplace).
- According to Leroy Merlin's e-commerce head, Mirakl handled "everything from seller onboarding and product catalog creation to e-commerce platform integration and invoice generation" — [CIO España](https://www.cio.com/article/2519129/la-estrategia-de-leroy-merlin-en-los-mercados-b2c.html).
- The seller back office is Mirakl itself. To connect a tool you need an API key, a Store/Shop ID and a country choice (Portugal is one option). Sellers first pass a document check with a Leroy Merlin account manager — [e-tailize](https://e-tailize.com/help/marketplaces/sell-on-leroy-merlin/) (page last verified 7 Sept 2026). One vendor blog says the platform runs on "Mirakl Connect" — [eDesk ES](https://www.edesk.com/es/blog/como-vender-leroy-merlin) (secondary).
- ChannelEngine lists "LM PT" as a separate Leroy Merlin Portugal channel. A Leroy Merlin account manager must activate it before it appears — [ChannelEngine guide](https://support.channelengine.com/hc/en-us/articles/4700020245789-Leroy-Merlin-marketplace-guide). Channable says listings can be created only for the France, Italy, Spain and Portugal regions. Leroy Merlin and Bricoman (also ADEO) share one channel set-up — [Channable](https://helpcenter.channable.com/hc/en-us/articles/8505198082578-How-to-set-up-a-Leroy-Merlin-or-Bricoman-channel).
- The Spanish marketplace terms are issued by "ADEO Marketplace Services", which presents itself as an intermediary and "not a party to the Sale Contract". The version in force is dated October 2025 — [leroymerlin.es, Condiciones generales del marketplace](https://www.leroymerlin.es/condiciones-generales-de-uso-del-marketplace.html). The French version is dated 18 June 2026 — [leroymerlin.fr](https://www.leroymerlin.fr/conditions-d-utilisation-marketplace.html). The economic clauses did not appear in the snippets.

**Scale (Spain, with France as reference)**
- Spain launched in November 2022 with about 150 sellers and over 150,000 products — [Forbes España](https://forbes.es/ultima-hora/201701/leroy-merlin-lanza-su-marketplace-en-espana-que-contara-con-150-vendedores-y-mas-de-150-000-productos/); [Leroy Merlin corporate press release (PDF)](https://corporativo.leroymerlin.es/documents/38162/0/Leroy+Merlin+lanza+su+nuevo+marketplace+en+Espa%C3%B1a.pdf/316051b0-1757-1530-20b0-5a1615d7418e).
- About one year later (Nov 2023): more than 1 million products and 900 sellers, with "90% of active sellers having had sales". The article says the marketplace operates in Spain, France, Italy and Portugal — [Marketing4ecommerce](https://marketing4ecommerce.net/leroy-merlin-el-marketplace-del-millon-de-productos/). (It is unclear whether the 900 counts Spain only or several countries.)
- 2024 (Spain): marketplace sales of €96M and 1,600 sellers, 59% more than in 2023 — [Alimarket](https://www.alimarket.es/construccion/noticia/405511/leroy-merlin-invertira-500-m--hasta-2030-para-seguir-creciendo) (search-summary attribution).
- 2025 (Spain): marketplace contributed about €150M, up 53% — [Forbes España](https://forbes.es/economia/888961/leroy-merlin-gana-un-13-mas-en-espana-en-2025-en-un-entorno-volatil-e-incierto/). El Español gives about +50% — [El Español, 12 Mar 2026](https://www.elespanol.com/invertia/empresas/distribucion/20260312/leroy-merlin-dispara-beneficio-millones-supera-facturacion-espana/1003744166448_0.amp.html). **Conflict: +53% vs +50%.** Remote sales (which include the marketplace) were €417M (+25%). Total Spanish sales, Leroy Merlin plus Obramat, were €3,827M (+6.6%) — same sources.
- The Spanish web catalog has 4.5 million references, with a target of 5 million by the end of 2026. Investment is €500M over 5 years (€100M/yr) — [Alimarket](https://www.alimarket.es/construccion/noticia/405511/leroy-merlin-invertira-500-m--hasta-2030-para-seguir-creciendo).
- France (reference): more than 500 third-party sellers, and the marketplace broke even for the first time in January 2024 — [Le Monde Informatique](https://www.lemondeinformatique.fr/actualites/lire-les-dessous-it-de-la-marketplace-de-leroy-merlin-94437.html). eDesk claims more than 900 active sellers and more than 30M visitors/month in France — [eDesk FR](https://www.edesk.com/fr/blog/quel-est-le-meilleur-service-dassistance-pour-le-commerce-electronique-pour-les-vendeurs-de-leroy-merlin/). **Conflict: 500 vs 900 (different dates and sources).**
- Portugal: Leroy Merlin Portugal had €1,386M gross sales in 2025 (+9.8%), €70.2M profit, 47 stores and more than 6,200 employees. It has been in Portugal since 2003 — [Forbes Portugal](https://www.forbespt.com/?p=187438). **I found no Portugal-specific marketplace GMV, seller count or launch date.**

**Categories / seller profile**
- Sellers must be home-improvement or home-sector professionals registered in a commercial register, able to run delivery, customer service and after-sales. The marketplace is "specialised and selective" — [Lengow FR](https://www.lengow.com/fr/integrations/vendre-sur-la-marketplace-leroy-merlin); [Lengow IT](https://www.lengow.com/it/integrations/leroy-merlin). Lengow ES describes the profile as a registered company in home, garden or DIY — [Lengow ES](https://www.lengow.com/es/?p=67765).
- Brazil's goal (2018) was to widen the range into appliances, furniture and services without investing in storage or logistics, while earning commission — [Mirakl, Leroy Merlin Brazil](https://www.mirakl.com/content/customer-stories/leroy-merlin-brazil/). (Brazil, used only as an indication of the category strategy.)

**Fees**
- No setup fee. The monthly fee is €39 excl. VAT, plus a commission that varies by category — [ChannelEngine](https://support.channelengine.com/hc/en-us/articles/4700020245789-Leroy-Merlin-marketplace-guide). Lengow says €39/month excl. VAT, no commitment, plus a variable category commission **calculated including shipping costs**, with a typical go-live time of 2 to 6 weeks — [Lengow DE](https://www.lengow.com/de/integrationen/leroy-merlin); also [Lengow IT](https://www.lengow.com/it/integrations/leroy-merlin) and [e-tailize](https://e-tailize.com/help/marketplaces/sell-on-leroy-merlin/).
- No public per-category grid exists. eDesk puts DIY commissions in general at 10–20% and notes the per-category percentages are not widely published — [eDesk ES](https://www.edesk.com/es/blog/como-vender-leroy-merlin). The only fixed percentage found, 18% plus R$49/month, is for **Brazil** and does not apply to Iberia — [Bling blog](https://blog.bling.com.br/leroy-merlin-marketplace/).
- Benchmark for third-party tool pricing: e-tailize's own Leroy Merlin programme starts at €249/month — [e-tailize](https://e-tailize.com/help/marketplaces/sell-on-leroy-merlin/).

**Entry and catalog requirements**
- EU address, EU VAT number, EU stock, an EAN-13 for every product, and trademarks registered at INPI, WIPO or both. A carrier that delivers to homes and returns a tracking link. Orders must be ready within 45 days — [e-tailize](https://e-tailize.com/help/marketplaces/sell-on-leroy-merlin/). Leroy Merlin "accepts only products provided with EAN" — [BeezUp](https://help.beezup.com/en/articles/5685474-leroy-merlin-products-publication).
- "Leroy Merlin does not publish its content guide. The rules that count sit per category in your own Mirakl back office." A category can reject a value it doesn't accept. Leroy Merlin gives each product a **completeness score**, and while the score stays under the threshold the product is taken offline. The score is visible only in the Mirakl back office — [e-tailize content rules](https://e-tailize.com/help/marketplaces/content-rules-for-leroy-merlin/).
- Images at least 500×500 px, at most 10 MB, preferably without text. Long descriptions up to 5,000 characters, with simple HTML (br, p). Map as many attributes as possible for better display — [ChannelEngine](https://support.channelengine.com/hc/en-us/articles/4700020245789-Leroy-Merlin-marketplace-guide) (third-party guide; date not shown).
- Multi-country: titles and texts use country-specific i18n attributes per market. Do not fill the English title until the UK market opens — [Lengow help, LM Mirakl attributes, multi-country](https://help.lengow.com/hc/articles/9150658882588-Leroy-Merlin-Mirakl-Attributes-Multi-country-settings). Leroy Merlin's category tree is a Mirakl hierarchy — [Lengow help, LM Mirakl categories](https://help.lengow.com/hc/en-us/articles/9150641723292-Leroy-Merlin-Mirakl-Categories).
- Category-specific attributes (for example dimensions for furniture). For single products, SellerArticleId and SellerProductId must have the same value. A GPSR section sits in the shared attributes — [Channable](https://helpcenter.channable.com/hc/en-us/articles/8505198082578-How-to-set-up-a-Leroy-Merlin-or-Bricoman-channel). GPSR compliance on Leroy Merlin needs company-data verification and a **self-certification in the Seller Portal** — [ChannelEngine GPSR](https://support.channelengine.com/hc/en-us/articles/22805358255773).
- Brands can also feed product content to Leroy Merlin (France, Italy, Spain, Portugal) through a PIM connector — [Contentserv connector](https://marketplace.contentserv.com/connectors/leroy-merlin-connector).

**Logistics, delivery, returns, omnichannel**
- The seller ships and must use a home-delivery carrier with tracking — [e-tailize](https://e-tailize.com/help/marketplaces/sell-on-leroy-merlin/). BigBuy's connector advises keeping "as many carriers active as possible". The default preparation time is 3 days and can be changed — [BigBuy Academy](https://www.bigbuy.eu/academy/en/how-to-synchronise-leroy-merlin-through-the-multi-channel-integration-platform/).
- Spain returns: marketplace products have a **14-day return window from delivery**, and the request goes **directly to the seller**, not to Leroy Merlin. The order email shows "productos vendidos por" Leroy Merlin or a different seller — [leroymerlin.es FAQ, Devoluciones](https://www.leroymerlin.es/preguntas-frecuentes/devoluciones-y-reembolsos/). One seller's own terms accept only complete returns in original packaging, may charge the customer for return shipping, and cap delivery at 30 days unless agreed otherwise — [leroymerlin.es seller page](https://www.leroymerlin.es/vendedor/silamp.html) (a single seller's terms, not a Leroy Merlin rule).
- In Portugal, a buyer quotes Leroy Merlin's marketplace conditions: "the seller will indicate the method and carrier for the return" — [Portal da Queixa complaint](https://portaldaqueixa.com/brands/leroy-merlin/complaints/leroy-merlin-dificuldades-no-processo-de-devolucao-de-produto-comprado-online-162240626) (a 160×200 bed sold by the partner Doméo).
- France (official): the marketplace is "online-oriented", so **store returns and store pickup are not possible** for marketplace products. They earn no loyalty points, and the external seller handles customer service — [leroymerlin.fr, explication marketplace](https://www.leroymerlin.fr/explication-marketplace.html). I found no equivalent official page for Spain or Portugal. The Spanish app advertises 2-hour store pickup and 24-hour home delivery for the general catalog without mentioning marketplace items — [mwm.ai app listing](https://mwm.ai/es/apps/leroy-merlin/370400343).
- Leroy Merlin's **own** (first-party) last mile in Spain uses partner carriers chosen by weight, volume and delivery mode, with Correos Express typically for small parcels — [ordertracker](https://www.ordertracker.com/es/transportistas/leroy-merlin) (this describes Leroy Merlin's own orders, not seller rules).

**KPIs and payment terms**
- I found no published Leroy Merlin seller KPI thresholds (acceptance, late shipment, cancellation, incident rate, reviews) in any searched source. Searches in English, Spanish and French all returned nothing official — see Gaps.
- Reference point from another Mirakl operator (not Iberian): Best Buy's Mirakl seller standards, effective 18 May 2026, are incident rate ≤4%, late shipment ≤5% and acceptance ≥95%. They also track acceptance time, tracking rate, late delivery, return rate and response time, and sellers must meet them "to avoid suspension" — [Best Buy Marketplace KPIs](https://partners.bestbuy.com/-/seller-hub/set-up-your-store/guidelinesfaqs/defs-key-perf-indicators).

### Inferences
- Leroy Merlin's Mirakl set-up almost certainly applies the standard Mirakl seller metrics (acceptance rate and time, incident rate, valid tracking, message response time), as PcComponentes does openly (see section 3). The exact thresholds are operator-set and hidden in the back office. This is an inference from the platform, not evidence.
- The completeness score with automatic de-listing is the most important catalog mechanism for an SMB. A product can be "live" in the seller's feed and invisible on the site, and the seller only finds out by logging into the Mirakl back office.
- The marketplace is about 36% of Leroy Merlin Spain's remote sales (€150M / €417M) and about 4% of total Spanish sales (€150M / €3,827M) in 2025. It is small but growing about 50% a year, which suggests continued seller recruitment in Spain.
- A commission computed on price plus shipping matters especially for bulky DIY items, where freight is a large share of the basket. Sellers who price shipping separately still pay commission on it.
- Portugal's marketplace is probably much smaller than Spain's (no numbers are published, and the Portuguese press did not cover it). It is a channel to add once a seller is already on Spain or France, since the same Mirakl account and offer structure are reused per country.

### Gaps
- Leroy Merlin's KPI thresholds and penalty ladder (warnings, suspension, de-listing) are not public. Neither are the commission grid by category, the payout cycle or payment terms (none found), or the delivery-promise rules for bulky goods (two-person delivery, delivery-slot booking, maximum lead time beyond the "ready within 45 days" figure).
- Portugal marketplace launch date, seller count and GMV: not found.
- Whether Spain or Portugal allow store pickup or store returns for marketplace items: only the French policy (no) was found.
- Energy-label, CE or technical-sheet (ficha técnica) requirements for DIY categories: no source mentioned them explicitly beyond GPSR.
- Leroy Merlin Spain's October 2025 terms could not be opened (WebFetch blocked), so the fee and KPI clauses that may be inside them could not be read.

## 2. Pains Leroy Merlin sellers report (catalog onboarding, bulky logistics, customer friction)

### Takeaway
No independent seller testimonials (forums, LinkedIn) surfaced. The evidence comes from integrator content describing onboarding friction, and from consumer complaints (Portugal and Spain, 2024–2026) that show the operational failures sellers are blamed for: late deliveries, cancellations after payment for lack of stock, refused or uncoordinated returns, and products not matching the listing. The main structural pains are opaque category rules and completeness scores, strict data requirements (EAN, trademark, GPSR), seller-run bulky logistics and returns, and responsibility being pushed onto the seller.

### Cited Findings
- **Catalog errors can close the account:** "As a reseller it is not really easy to avoid errors in the product catalogue. And beware: marketplace errors can quickly lead to closure of the reseller's account." Each marketplace has its own requirements and formatting — [Lengow FR, marketplace errors in home and garden](https://www.lengow.com/fr/ressources/erreurs-marketplace-maison-jardin); [Lengow FR](https://www.lengow.com/fr/integrations/vendre-sur-la-marketplace-leroy-merlin).
- **Opaque, per-category rules:** there is no published content guide, the rules sit per category in the Mirakl back office, values can be rejected, and the completeness score that triggers de-listing is visible only in Mirakl — [e-tailize content rules](https://e-tailize.com/help/marketplaces/content-rules-for-leroy-merlin/).
- **Hard entry barriers for small resellers:** EAN-13 on every SKU and a registered trademark (INPI/WIPO) — [e-tailize](https://e-tailize.com/help/marketplaces/sell-on-leroy-merlin/); EAN-only products — [BeezUp](https://help.beezup.com/en/articles/5685474-leroy-merlin-products-publication).
- **Slow onboarding:** a document check with an account manager — [e-tailize](https://e-tailize.com/help/marketplaces/sell-on-leroy-merlin/); the Portugal channel must be activated by an account manager — [ChannelEngine](https://support.channelengine.com/hc/en-us/articles/4700020245789-Leroy-Merlin-marketplace-guide); 2 to 6 weeks to go live — [Lengow DE](https://www.lengow.com/de/integrationen/leroy-merlin).
- **Late delivery leading to refused returns (Portugal, 2026):** a seller refused a return of a shelving unit, saying the return window had expired, after the seller itself had delivered late — [Portal da Queixa](https://portaldaqueixa.com/brands/leroy-merlin/complaints/leroy-merlin-recusa-de-devolurecusa-de-devolucao-injustificada-apos-atraso-na-entrega-de-produto-marketplacecao-de-estante-devido-a-atraso-na-entrega-161380026).
- **Return logistics not arranged by the seller (Portugal):** the seller was supposed to indicate the return method and carrier for a bed and did not — [Portal da Queixa](https://portaldaqueixa.com/brands/leroy-merlin/complaints/leroy-merlin-dificuldades-no-processo-de-devolucao-de-produto-comprado-online-162240626).
- **Delivery promise vs reality:** a buyer reports repeated failures with no concrete delivery date, while the same product from the same partner was still listed with delivery in "6 working days" — [Portal da Queixa](https://portaldaqueixa.com/brands/leroy-merlin/complaints/leroy-merlin-sucessivos-incumprimentos-e-informacao-contraditoria-de-artigo-comprado-em-marketplace-163360626).
- **Stock-out cancellations after payment:** orders cancelled after payment for lack of stock — [Portal da Queixa](https://portaldaqueixa.com/brands/leroy-merlin/complaints/leroy-merlin-cancelamento-de-encomenda-apos-pagamento-devido-a-falta-de-stock-157942926). A 2024 Spanish case of a seller cancelling a delayed order appears on [OCU](https://www.ocu.org/reclamar/lista-reclamaciones-publicas/cancelaci-C3-B3n-pedido-por-demora-/ca60b2f7f6f8f9b789).
- **Products not as described:** a product received did not match the listing, and there was no support with the return — [Portal da Queixa](https://portaldaqueixa.com/brands/leroy-merlin/complaints/leroy-merlin-produto-recebido-nao-corresponde-ao-anunciado-e-falta-de-apoio-na-devolucao-160301226).
- **Responsibility pushed onto the seller and 1P/3P confusion:** in Spain, customer service referred a buyer to the external seller because Leroy Merlin "was not the real seller" — [OCU complaint](https://www.ocu.org/reclamar/lista-reclamaciones-publicas/leroy-merlin-no-cumple-los-pla/e7d91e7d52600f2acf) (search-summary attribution among several OCU complaints). A French state-platform testimony says Leroy Merlin put 100% of the blame for a non-delivery on the third-party seller — [plus.transformation.gouv.fr](https://www.plus.transformation.gouv.fr/experiences/6048965_livraison-non-arrivee-dans-les-delais). Official policy: for any incident the buyer must contact only the external seller — [leroymerlin.fr](https://www.leroymerlin.fr/explication-marketplace.html).
- **Brand-level reputation signal (Portugal):** Portal da Queixa scores Leroy Merlin overall at 56.1, "Razoável" (fair), with a 97.9% response rate and a 61.9% resolution rate over 12 months — [Portal da Queixa brand data](https://portaldaqueixa.com/brands/leroy-merlin/complaints/leroy-merlin-dificuldades-no-processo-de-devolucao-de-produto-comprado-online-162240626) (all of Leroy Merlin, not marketplace-only).
- **No logistics tooling from the operator (Brazil, 2025):** Leroy Merlin "does not offer tools for e-commerce management and logistics processes", so the seller needs its own logistics — [Bling blog](https://blog.bling.com.br/leroy-merlin-marketplace/) (Brazil, by analogy only).

### Inferences
- These consumer complaints are the visible side of the seller KPIs. Every late shipment, cancellation, unanswered message or refused return becomes an "incident" that feeds the incident rate, which on Mirakl marketplaces drives automatic warnings and suspensions (see the PcComponentes mechanism in section 3). For an SMB selling bulky DIY items, missing the delivery promise is the main KPI risk.
- Bulky items make the operational pains sharper: freight is expensive (and commissioned, see section 1), returns of large items are costly and the seller must arrange the carrier, and carriers for large items have longer and less reliable lead times than parcel networks. A hub that cannot set per-SKU, per-channel lead times and carrier rules will produce exactly the "promised 6 working days" failures seen above.
- Cancellations after payment point to stock not being synchronised between the seller's own shop, warehouse and other marketplaces. This is the core multichannel inventory problem.

### Gaps
- No first-hand seller testimonials (LinkedIn posts, seller forums, case studies naming Iberian SMB sellers) were found within the search budget.
- No quantification of how many sellers fail onboarding, how long catalog approval takes per category, or what share of listings are de-listed by the completeness score.

## 3. Other Iberian marketplaces: Mirakl vs own platform, KPIs, fees, catalog rules, logistics, integration route (comparison)

### Takeaway
Leroy Merlin, El Corte Inglés, PcComponentes, MediaMarkt and Carrefour Spain all run on Mirakl (as does Worten, covered earlier). One Mirakl seller-API connector can reach all of them, but each operator has its own category tree, attributes, value lists and KPI thresholds. Fnac is a hybrid: Mirakl for products, in-house systems for offers and orders. Miravia, Temu, Shein and ManoMano run their own platforms and need separate integrations. Where fees are published, Mirakl operators cluster around €39–€39.99/month plus a category commission (typically quoted at 8–20%). Miravia charges no monthly fee. Temu's local programme is reported at about 8–15% all-in.

## 4. Which marketplaces matter most for Portuguese vs Spanish SMBs

### Takeaway
For Portuguese SMBs the domestic core is Worten, Fnac PT, KuantoKusta and El Corte Inglés PT (plus Amazon.es, Temu and AliExpress by traffic). Leroy Merlin PT and PcComponentes PT are niche add-ons that reuse Spanish Mirakl set-ups. Carrefour, PcComponentes, MediaMarkt and Leroy Merlin are vertical options (electronics, DIY/home), and Miravia and Shein matter for fashion and lifestyle.

## 5. Which of these pains a multichannel SaaS hub (PIM + stock/WMS + orders + metrics + Ads + AI) could solve

### Takeaway
Most of the documented pains are data and operations problems a hub can attack directly: per-operator catalog mapping and completeness, EAN, trademark and GPSR compliance fields, multilingual PT/ES content, stock sync to avoid post-payment cancellations, per-SKU lead-time and carrier rules for bulky goods, KPI monitoring before suspension thresholds, a unified customer-message inbox with SLA timers, returns orchestration, and per-channel profitability including fixed fees and commission on shipping. A hub cannot fix opaque operator rules, account-manager gating, or the operator's habit of pushing responsibility onto the seller. It can only surface them early.

### Cited Findings
- **Pain: opaque category rules and hidden completeness score that triggers de-listing** — [e-tailize content rules](https://e-tailize.com/help/marketplaces/content-rules-for-leroy-merlin/). Catalog errors can lead to account closure — [Lengow FR](https://www.lengow.com/fr/ressources/erreurs-marketplace-maison-jardin).
- **Pain: hard data prerequisites** (EAN-13, registered trademark, GPSR self-certification, manufacturer/importer data) — [e-tailize](https://e-tailize.com/help/marketplaces/sell-on-leroy-merlin/), [ChannelEngine GPSR](https://support.channelengine.com/hc/en-us/articles/22805358255773), [Channable Carrefour](https://helpcenter.channable.com/list-advertise/list-on-marketplaces/mirakl-marketplaces/setting-up-a-carrefour-spain-api).

- **Pain: stock-out cancellations after payment, and delivery promise not met** — [Portal da Queixa](https://portaldaqueixa.com/brands/leroy-merlin/complaints/leroy-merlin-cancelamento-de-encomenda-apos-pagamento-devido-a-falta-de-stock-157942926), [Portal da Queixa](https://portaldaqueixa.com/brands/leroy-merlin/complaints/leroy-merlin-sucessivos-incumprimentos-e-informacao-contraditoria-de-artigo-comprado-em-marketplace-163360626).

### Inferences
Pain-to-module mapping (all inference, built on the findings above):

| Pain (evidence above) | Hub module | Concrete feature |
|---|---|---|
| Per-operator category trees, value lists, rejected values | PIM | Per-operator attribute templates synced from each Mirakl operator's hierarchy and value lists; pre-validation before sending; parsing of the operator's import error reports into human-readable fixes |
| Completeness score visible only in the Mirakl back office | PIM + Metrics | A per-channel completeness score in the hub; alerts for "live in feed but not visible on site" |
| EAN-13, trademark, GPSR and DSA manufacturer/importer data | PIM (compliance) | GTIN check-digit validation, brand and trademark evidence stored per brand, a GPSR block (manufacturer, EU responsible person, safety docs) reused across all operators |
| PT/ES (and FR/IT) country-specific text fields | PIM + AI | AI translation and adaptation of titles and descriptions per market, with operator length and HTML rules (for example 5,000 characters on Leroy Merlin) |
| Image rules (≥500 px, no text, ≤10 MB) | PIM (DAM) | Automatic image checks and resizing per channel |
| Post-payment stock-out cancellations | Stock/WMS | One stock pool across PrestaShop and all marketplaces, with per-channel safety buffers and near-real-time offer quantity updates |
| Delivery-promise misses on bulky items | Orders + WMS | Per-SKU, per-channel lead time and carrier profile by weight and volume; prep-time settings pushed to each operator (BigBuy's 3-day default shows this is configurable) |
| Seller-arranged returns of large items; disputes over return windows | Orders (RMA) | Returns workflow with carrier booking, return-window clock that starts at actual delivery date, evidence log |
| KPI-driven automatic suspension (PcComponentes: ≥2 weeks, payouts blocked) | Metrics + AI | Per-operator KPI dashboard (acceptance, incident rate, tracking, response time) with forecast-to-threshold alerts; auto-acceptance rules; mandatory tracking upload |
| 48 h message SLA (Carrefour) and similar | Orders (inbox) | A unified inbox across Mirakl operators with SLA timers and AI reply drafts in PT/ES |
| €39–€39.99/month per channel plus category commission including shipping | Metrics + AI decision support | Per-channel P&L per SKU (fee share, commission on price plus shipping, freight, returns); "should I list this SKU on channel X" recommendations |
| Account-manager gating, opaque rules, operator pushing blame onto the seller | Not solvable | The hub can only document evidence (timestamps, tracking) for disputes |

- Positioning: incumbents (Lengow, ChannelEngine, e-tailize at €249+/month) are feed and order connectors. A hub that bundles PIM, stock, a Mirakl multi-operator connector and KPI monitoring at an SMB price point (well under €249/month) is differentiated mainly on (a) Portuguese language and Portuguese carriers, (b) one stock pool with PrestaShop, and (c) proactive KPI and profitability alerts.

### Gaps
- Whether the Mirakl seller API exposes operator-specific KPI values and product completeness scores to third-party tools (needed for the alerting features) was not verified in this run. Check Mirakl's seller API documentation.
- Which Portuguese and Spanish carriers handle bulky and two-person delivery for marketplace sellers, and their API availability, was not researched.
